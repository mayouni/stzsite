## Made of three things {#made}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>The domain: its being</h3><p>The world's things, what is true of them, and how they relate. Things are nodes and relations are edges, so the domain is a graph. Truth is a conscience and not a syntax: each thing declares what should hold, such as a balance that is never below zero, and validity is part of the world's being. Meaning is first-class: <i>an account belongs to a client</i> is written as knowledge.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>The life: its becoming</h3><p>What the world does and what it wants. Its behaviour is how it reacts when something changes. Its purpose is a goal that does not say what to do and says what should become true, with plans that pursue it.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>The body: its embodiment</h3><p>Where the world endures: a graph, files, memory. A world's substance persists as the graph it is. The governed store beneath it is <a href="store.html">told on its own page</a>.</p></div>
</div>

Strip any of the three and there is no world.

## Met from outside in four ways {#met}

<ol class="steps">
<li value="1"><b>Seen.</b> The world has a presence: it can be looked at, in whatever surface.</li>
<li value="2"><b>Engaged.</b> It has an intent: a person or an agent can ask it something and do something with it.</li>
<li value="3"><b>Tuned.</b> It can be refined: changed under governance, with the change judged before it lands.</li>
<li value="4"><b>Reached.</b> It has a reach: it appears on a surface, on the web today and elsewhere as the <a href="surfaces.html">surfaces</a> are built.</li>
</ol>

Strip these four and the world still lives, headless. That is why one construct can be a business tool, an agent, a game or a simulation.

## Built from what already exists {#organs}

The construct animates organs that exist elsewhere in the library: the graph, the workflow, the reactive engine, the Softanzuter's ladder, goals and plans, data, and the application server. See <a href="softanzuter.html">the Softanzuter</a> and <a href="host.html">the host</a>.
