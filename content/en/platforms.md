## What it means {#means}

Most organisations do not need one more application. A bank has a tool for its field staff, a console for its back office and a portal for its customers. A restaurant has the customer's phone, the kitchen's screen and the owner's workshop. Each is a different person on a different device, yet they share the same customers, the same rules and the same identity. The usual answer splits this into separate products glued together, or squeezes it into one host that runs small apps from a store.

Softanza's answer is that such a platform is **a constellation of worlds**. Each world is an application, a living world of meaning. The worlds share a common ground: identity, data, services. Declared bonds join them, rules govern every crossing, and the whole is governed as one. A world inside a constellation can itself be a constellation, so the same construction scales from a two-screen tool to a national platform.

<p class="way"><span>The Softanza way</span> A platform is not a host that runs other people's apps. It is a constellation of worlds declared in the organisation's own words, sharing one ground, joined by bonds, and governed as one.</p>

Every platform built this way declares the same five things, as text it owns: its shared ground, a world for each person and device, the bonds between the worlds, its rules and its roles. What changes from one platform to the next is only the content: a catalogue and its taxes, a bank's regulations, an organisation's circuit of approvals, the languages its people speak.

What every one receives is the same. A grammar, and the court that judges every sentence written in it. Projection to the web, the phone, the desktop, the server and the device. Data that moves through stages with an audit trail and undo. And an agent that stands on the knowledge the platform declares, held to the same rules as the people. The platform is Softanza's; the words are the organisation's.

## Honest about the stage {#stage}

- **The construction is built.** A world and a constellation of worlds run in the library, and the runs above come from their guard.
- **One shell already carries several domains.** In Softanza's applications, the same shell holds domain packs from banking to an NGO's due diligence: the same shell, a different domain, no code change.
- **The customer platforms run today on ordinary web stacks, and DIKO Hub is not built.** Their worlds, bonds and rules are written, or being written, as Softanza declarations, so moving them onto the Softanza engine is a change of engine, not of plan.
- **No platform writes its guarantee sheet yet.** The north star asks every solution for five rows, one per system it answers to: technical, social, ecological, economic and cultural. That is the next step for each of them.
