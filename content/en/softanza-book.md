## The book {#book}

<figure class="book-cover"><a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/en/"><img src="../assets/img/book-cover-en.webp" width="640" height="837" alt="The cover of the book: AYOUNI, Softanza, The Governed Software Platform of the AI Age, Mansour Ayouni; an elder in a turban drawn in coloured facets."></a></figure>

<p class="proof">Mansour Ayouni, <i><a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/en/">Softanza: The Governed Software Platform of the AI Age</a></i> (second of the AYOUNI books), AYOUNI, 2026, draft.</p>

<p class="cta"><a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/en/">Read it in English</a><a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/fr/">Lire en français</a></p>

The book rethinks programming, languages and intelligence from first principles, and it is about the platform as a whole: the engine, the language Haro, the courts that judge what is declared, and the place of the AI model, which comes last. It is the reference the pages of this site are the working proof of.

It is a book that runs. Every code block in it is run inside the Softanza library at a named commit and judged against the promise lines it carries, and a chapter whose promises are not kept fails its build.

## Where it stands {#stage}

<p class="proof"><b>in construction</b> The book is a draft: 42 pages in all, and every page carries the banner "Draft · not yet read by the author", with its French counterpart on the French pages. It is read online as a web reader, <a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/en/">in English</a> and <a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/fr/">in French</a>, and published as data too: <a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/books/softanza/book.json">the manifest</a> lists every page, and <a href="https://mayouni.github.io/ayouni/llms.txt">llms.txt</a> gives an AI agent the way in. The source repository is public: <a href="https://github.com/mayouni/softanza-book">mayouni/softanza-book</a>. No PDF and no ePub is online.</p>
