<div class="cards principles">

<div class="card"><h3>1 · Declare a language</h3><p><b>Declare yours, not ours.</b> In the agentic era you do not write software: you declare worlds in languages made for their domains, and you govern how those worlds change. Declaring a language is as easy as declaring a variable.</p><ul><li>five languages declared before the act was named: query, interface, expression, meta-grammar, workflow</li><li>every declared grammar emits its constraint grammar: an agent cannot utter a malformed sentence</li><li>three strata per language: a closed syntax, a knowledge-graph semantics, a conversational pragmatics</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>2 · Everything is judged</h3><p><b>Nothing is believed.</b> A narration runs as it is read and stores no output; a promise is checked by running; a guard narrates its scene and counts its assertions. What did not run is not shown.</p><ul><li>134 run-verified narrations in <a href="https://github.com/mayouni/stzlib/tree/main/libraries/stzlib/base/doc/narrations">doc/narrations</a></li><li>narrated guards as specifications, with numpy and LAPACK as oracles and negative controls</li><li>this site obeys the same law: every code block was run on the night of publication</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>3 · One engine, many faces</h3><p><b>Substance lives in the engine.</b> Strings, exact numbers, tables, graphs, maps, GPU, sound, neural networks, security: all of it is written in Zig, in one engine. The language you write, Haro, is its face.</p><ul><li><a href="https://github.com/mayouni/stzlib/tree/main/libraries/stzlib/engine/src">401 Zig source files</a> in the engine</li><li>28 areas, 334 lanes rated in <a href="atlas.html">the Atlas</a></li><li>one C interface, so that every future face shares the same engine</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>4 · Agents that cannot hurt you</h3><p><b>The model proposes; only a governed actor commits.</b> A language model never holds the capability to act. Work rehearses in a workbench with no reference to reality, and the plan that comes out faces a court.</p><ul><li><a href="https://github.com/mayouni/stzlib/blob/main/libraries/stzlib/base/security/SOFTANZA_THREAT_MODEL.md">38 guarantees</a>, each tied to a guard</li><li>detection in 395 ms, containment in 50 ms, measured tonight</li><li>an agent proposed 610 deletions; none was committed</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>5 · Knowledge of your world</h3><p><b>Wolfram knows the world's facts. Softanza knows your world.</b> Your organisation's entities, rules, actors and flows, declared in plain text, and the ledger of what happened under them: the dependency graph and the symbol table are the same graph.</p><ul><li>one knowledge file describes a restaurant, a bank, a school; adding a fact adds intelligence with no code change</li><li>the natural layer answers about your world, not about the periodic table</li><li>what we do not do: symbolic mathematics and the curated corpus of the world</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>6 · Code that reads</h3><p><b>The human is the parser.</b> A line reads like a sentence: find first, then apply. Names are verbs, forms say what they do, and a natural-language instruction runs.</p><ul><li><code>o1.ContainsDuplicates()</code> reads as it is written</li><li>a string knows its 5,384 methods and can explain each one</li><li>"Create a list with [ 5, 3, 5, 1 ] and remove its duplicates" answered <code>[ 5, 3, 1 ]</code></li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>7 · In your language</h3><p><b>The course speaks English, French, Arabic and Hausa.</b> The engine counts characters, not bytes; languages name themselves; the same instruction runs in the four languages.</p><ul><li>two courses of fifteen chapters, each in four languages, every cell run</li><li>"Yi jeri dauke [ 5, 3, 5, 1 ] cire maimaitattu" answered <code>[ 5, 3, 1 ]</code></li><li>Zarma is not there yet: it is an invitation</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>8 · Exact by default</h3><p><b>A number knows in which form it is held.</b> Integers are exact at any size, decimals say they are decimal, and the algebra is proven against an oracle.</p><ul><li><code>Q(2).Power(64)</code> answers 18446744073709551616</li><li>LAPACK-class decompositions, proven against a κ·eps oracle</li><li>automatic differentiation checks a hand-written gradient</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>9 · Honest by design</h3><p><b>A page that shows its gaps is believed on its strengths.</b> The Atlas rates every lane against the leaders of its category and shows the low ratings beside the high ones. Every product carries its stage. No figure without a file.</p><ul><li>101 Strong · 129 Solid · 68 Partial · 36 Emerging, over 334 lanes</li><li>the stages: built, charter, specification, ratified proposal, named</li><li>this site quotes no number of countries and no number of developers</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>10 · Sovereign by construction</h3><p><b>Nothing anyone else can withdraw.</b> The engine, the virtual machine, the declared operating system: every dependency gets a sovereignty verdict, owned, borrowed and pinned, or vendored with the code.</p><ul><li>Harobanda, the declared machine: one file describes the whole computer and every boot is checked against it</li><li>the engine builds for Windows, Linux and macOS from source</li><li>this site's fonts are hosted here; it opens without a network</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>11 · Programming by heart</h3><p><b>What you think is what you write.</b> The mission has not moved since 2018: an inclusive path, whatever the background, from an idea to a running system. The court is severe so that the surface can stay warm.</p><ul><li>a tutor that asks the missing question and never gives the answer</li><li>wise coding: analyse, induce, validate, and only then act</li><li>narration as the voice of every court</li></ul></div>

<div class="card"><h3>12 · Born in Africa</h3><p><b>Useful to the world.</b> Designed between Tunisia, Niamey and Paris, with a bank, a customs school and a restaurant as references; its machine is named after a bridge over the Niger.</p><ul><li>the density map of Niger, rendered by the engine from the official borders and the 2012 census</li><li>a Hausa edition of the course, awaiting its native review</li><li><a href="africa.html">the Africa page</a></li></ul></div>
