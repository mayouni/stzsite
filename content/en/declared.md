<p class="way"><span>The Softanza way</span> A language is named by its file, and a file is judged by a court when it is loaded. What you write is plain text you own, and a declaration that the court refuses never runs.</p>

<p class="proof"><b>in construction</b> Each row is a reading of the library and of the charters and READMEs of the estate's other repositories, by an outside assessment on 2026-10-07, and not a run. The grammar and the court of each language are told on the page in the last column; where there is no such page yet, the row is the whole account.</p>
