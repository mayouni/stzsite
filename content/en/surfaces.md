## What exists today {#today}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>The web <b>(built)</b></h3><p>The plan of record is plain: the web tier remains the delivery path for business applications. The applications that run use web-based tools, with a vendored toolkit and no build step, installed as an app on a phone, opened in a desktop shell or an Android shell.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>The native interface <b>(in construction)</b></h3><p>An engine above graphics, with an interface authored declaratively as text, right-to-left writing and accessibility as its standouts. Its order of attack is in-scene and game surfaces first, and operator-facing business software only when accessibility and input methods are funded as multi-year work.</p></div>
</div>

## What are directions {#directions}

- <b>A terminal</b>, for the people and the agents who work in one.
- <b>Voice</b>: speech synthesis exists in the engine; speech recognition and telephony do not.
- <b>Messaging</b>, the app already on a person's phone: told on <a href="conversations.html">the conversations page</a>, designed and mostly absent.
- <b>Devices</b>: a machine declared as software first, on <a href="sovereignty.html">the sovereignty page</a>.

<p class="way"><span>The Softanza way</span> The interface grammar is closed: a new surface adds renderings and never verbs. Every surface here is judged by the same <a href="zui.html">interface law</a>, whether it is a page, a window or a thread of messages.</p>

<p class="proof"><b>in construction</b> The native plane's renderer and its accessibility tree are read from the library's files and its eight guards; the plane was read and not seen on a screen for this page. The packaged phone shell and the desktop web view are absent from what the envelope generates today; the applications that run carry their own.</p>
