## Three grounds {#grounds}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>RestoLean, Lyon <b>(built, first release)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> a platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant. <b>What exists:</b> a mission of analysis in 2024, specification stories written as narrations, then an application that runs, shown on a real phone by the owner himself; its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. <b>Learned:</b> a specification is not a delivery.</p><p class="rx-src"><a href="forged.html#restolean">What the library learned</a></p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>Organizium, Niamey <b>(built, in use)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> to model a bank's organisation before its application, and to judge it by its own chart. <b>What exists:</b> the audit, the referential, a training module, and the Standard Edition under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, with the regulators' rules as validators of the chart. <b>Learned:</b> an organisation can be judged by its own chart.</p><p class="rx-src"><a href="forged.html#organizium">What the library learned</a> · <a href="customers.html">the reference's document</a></p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>DIKO, Niamey <b>(design study)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> a platform that speaks an organisation's own language. <b>What exists:</b> a design study, not a delivery. <b>Learned:</b> it caused the payments port the library now has.</p><p class="rx-src"><a href="forged.html#diko">What the library learned</a></p></div>
</div>

<p class="proof"><b>in construction</b> Nothing else is a reference until a document proves it, and a project is listed with its stage and never with the number of users or countries it is said to reach. Each ground ends, on the Forged page, with what it did not prove.</p>
