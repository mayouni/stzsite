## What would be asked, and what would be promised {#intent}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>What a partner would commit to <b>(designed)</b></h3><p>The laws that every customer is promised: ownership of everything in plain text, honesty about what is built and what is not, and no lock-in. A partner who breaks them is not a Softanza partner.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>What a partner could say <b>(designed)</b></h3><p>The one-sentence definition of the platform, the proofs on this site, and the stage word of each thing, which is the same for everyone. Decks per audience do not exist yet.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>What a partner could expect <b>(designed)</b></h3><p>The name of the project beside theirs where they delivered, introductions, and training. The terms of any revenue are not set, so none is stated here.</p></div>
</div>

## How to write {#write}

The same door as for everything else: <a href="https://github.com/mayouni/stzlib/issues">the issues of the repository</a>. A person or a company that wants to deliver with Softanza is invited to read <a href="journey-architect.html">the platform architect's journey</a> first, since it is the work a partner would do.

<p class="proof"><b>designed</b> No partner programme exists. Nothing on this page is offered, priced or promised beyond the three laws above, and any commitment the project has already made to an organisation is its own and is not altered by this page. The money question has one stated doctrine: the project funds itself, so that no outside capital acquires a voice over the open foundation. It is on <a href="offering.html#funding">the Audiences page</a>.</p>
