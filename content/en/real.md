## Three grounds {#grounds}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>Organizium, Niamey <b>(built, in use)</b></h3><p>A bank's organisation modelled before its application: its platforms, its security, its norms. The Standard Edition runs under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, and the regulators' rules, the BCEAO's and Basel III's, are validators of its chart. See <a href="customers.html">customers</a>.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>RestoLean, Lyon <b>(built, first release)</b></h3><p>A platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant and demonstrated on a real phone. Its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. A constellation with a commons and several worlds, one for the customer's phone. See <a href="forged.html">forged in projects</a>.</p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>DIKO Hub <b>(designed)</b></h3><p>A design study in Niger that links an organisation's tools. It is not a delivery. What it asked of the library is the payments port, which exists because of it. See <a href="forged.html#diko">forged in projects</a>.</p></div>
</div>

<p class="proof"><b>in construction</b> Nothing else is a reference until a document proves it. A project is listed with its stage and not with the number of users or countries it is said to reach.</p>
