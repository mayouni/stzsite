## Three projects {#grounds}

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h3>Organizium, Niamey <b>(built, in use)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> to model a bank's organisation before its application, and to judge it by its own chart: its platforms, its security, its norms. <b>What exists:</b> the audit, the referential, a training module, and the Standard Edition under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, with the regulators' rules, the BCEAO's and Basel III's, as validators of its chart. <b>Proved:</b> an organisation can be judged by its own chart.</p><p class="rx-src">Reference: the installation guide delivered to the bank · <a href="forged.html#organizium">what the library learned</a></p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>RestoLean, Lyon <b>(built, first release)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> a platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant. <b>What exists:</b> a mission of analysis in 2024, specification stories written as narrations, then an application that runs, shown on a real phone by the owner himself: a constellation with a commons and several worlds, one for the customer's phone. Its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. <b>Proved:</b> a specification is not a delivery.</p><p class="rx-src">Reference: amendment no. 1, closed on 4 August 2026 · <a href="forged.html#restolean">what the library learned</a></p></div>
<div class="card"><h3>DIKO, Niamey <b>(design study)</b></h3><p><b>Asked:</b> a platform that speaks an organisation's own language and links its tools. <b>What exists:</b> a design study, not a delivery. <b>Proved:</b> it caused the payments port the library now has.</p><p class="rx-src"><a href="forged.html#diko">What the library learned</a></p></div>
</div>

## The references {#references}

Two of the three are references, and each is attested by a document: the installation guide delivered to the Sonibank, and RestoLean's amendment no. 1. Nothing else is a reference until a document proves it. A project is listed with its stage, never with the number of users or countries it is said to reach, and this site quotes no number of developers.
