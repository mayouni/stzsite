## Four levels, each in its own place

| level | where | what it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the main menu, top, horizontal | five sections, short labels; Start is a button beside it, not a sixth entry |
| 2 | a horizontal bar under the menu, fixed on scroll | at most five pages of the section |
| 3 | a bar on the left, when the level-2 page has pages of its own | that page's family, or its generated list (areas, chapters, narrations, journeys, classes) |
| 4 | a bar on the right, only where a fourth level exists | the methods of a class, on a method's page |

The trail stays at the top of the page and ends on it (Rule 108). On a phone, levels 2 and 3 fold into one "Where you are"
line under the menu.
