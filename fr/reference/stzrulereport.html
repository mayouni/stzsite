Collect a GRAPH's own findings -- the graph checks ITSELF (CheckRules) and the report ingests. This is the Softanza-oriented gate: each graph owns its rules and reports itself; the report just gathers them across domains. oRep.Collect(oCodeGraph).Collect(oAgentGraph).Collect(oSecurityGraph) Any object answering CheckRules() qualifies (stzGraph, stzAgentGraph, stzSecurityGraph, stzOrgChart, stzCodeGraph).
