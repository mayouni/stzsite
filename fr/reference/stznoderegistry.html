Request/reply with a MANDATORY timeout. Returns the reply value; on anything else returns "" and LastStatus() says which anything: :Timeout (no reply in time -- at-most-once: it MAY have been handled), :Down (unreachable or died mid-wait), :Unknown (name never registered), :BadTimeout (a non-positive timeout is refused, infinite waits do not exist here).
