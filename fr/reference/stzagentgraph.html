Violations() below is the AUDIT -- it catches a bad graph after it is built. Grant() is the GATE: the governed way to add a capability, which REFUSES granting 'effectful' to an llm actor at the moment of expression, so the no-llm-effectful violation can never enter the graph through the sanctioned door.
