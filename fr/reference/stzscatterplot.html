The engine covers the plain plot -- points, both axes, their ticks and value labels, and the X/Y letters. Grid lines and per-point labels are still drawn by the Haro renderer below, and this defers to it rather than drawing something close: a plot that is nearly right raises nothing.

The Haro renderer this was ported from, kept so the guard can prove the two agree character for character -- and still used for grid and point labels.
