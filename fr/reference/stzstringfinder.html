Seventeen of this class's uses of @oString are `.Engine()` -- a number. Passing that number directly costs nothing, and the full stzString is rebuilt LAZILY (from the engine's own bytes) only for the handful of methods that need more: Content, NumberOfChars, IsEmpty, StartsWithCS, EndsWithCS, SubStrings, _FindSubStr.
