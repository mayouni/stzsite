IsPluralOfThisStzType()

IsPluralOfThisStzType(pcType): TRUE if content is the plural form of pcType (i.e. equal to pcType + "s").

Exemples de base (1)

Softanza
# stzchars is a plural of a stz type
? Q("stzchars").IsPluralOfAStzType()
# ... specifically of stzchar
? Q("stzchars").IsPluralOfThisStzType("stzchar")
