Référence · stzString

Find()

Find pcSubStr in the string: the positions of EVERY occurrence, as a list (engine-backed, codepoint-aware). (case-sensitive by default)

aussi: locate search for position of index of where is

Les formes de ce verbe

Find · FindW · FindZ · FindCS · FindST · FindXT · FindZZ · FindSTZ · FindWCS · FindSTZZ · FindZZCS

Exemples de base (1)

Softanza
o1 = new stzString("...emm...eh..emm...eh")
? @@( o1.FindMany([ "emm", "eh" ]) )
? @@(o1.FindManyAsSections([ "emm", "eh" ]))
? @@( o1.Find([ "emm", "eh" ]) )
? @@(o1.FindAsSections([ "emm", "eh" ])) # Of FindZZ() for short
