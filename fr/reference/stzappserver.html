Observe this server with a perf monitor (perf P3). From this call on, every request records its response time R into the timer 'http.request.ms', its arrival into the counter 'http.requests' (throughput X = its measured rate), and 5xx outcomes into 'http.errors'; GET /metrics serves the monitor's Prometheus exposition, and /health widens with the process senses.
