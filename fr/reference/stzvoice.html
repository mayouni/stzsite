WARMING UP ---------------------------------------------------------- Pay the cold cost NOW, not in the middle of the first sentence. VC0 measured a cold synthesis at 4.3x a warm one -- the same trap SN0 found in ma_device_start, where start-up took 500 ms and callbacks ran ahead of real time during it. SynthesisIsTheBottleneck
Synthesis is under one percent of the plane's output latency, so speech is not slow because it is computed -- it is late because the audio path is long. See the header, and the sound plan's S.5.
