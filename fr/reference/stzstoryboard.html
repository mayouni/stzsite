Sous chaque méthode, ses extensions (Q CS XT Z ZZ IB W U) : en gras et cochée, elle existe ; barrée, elle n'existe pas. Les extensions et leur sens

Aucune méthode de cette classe n'a encore d'exemple : la page ne sait pas encore construire un objet de cette classe, ni composer les arguments de ses méthodes.

ExpectFindings
A FRAME MAY BE ABOUT A PICTURE THE GATE FINDS FAULT WITH, and that is not a defect in the telling -- it is often the point. "Here is what goes wrong" is a frame whose picture is meant to be unlawful. So a frame says so, and then the gate's findings are its SUBJECT rather than its failure. A frame that expects findings and gets none is itself reported, because a flag nobody checks is a flag that rots.aucune extension

The caller supplies the fact; the storyboard still holds the caption to it, which is the whole point of binding rather than typing.QCSXTZZZIBWU
