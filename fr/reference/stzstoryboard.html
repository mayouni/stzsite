ExpectFindings
A FRAME MAY BE ABOUT A PICTURE THE GATE FINDS FAULT WITH, and that is not a defect in the telling -- it is often the point. "Here is what goes wrong" is a frame whose picture is meant to be unlawful. So a frame says so, and then the gate's findings are its SUBJECT rather than its failure. A frame that expects findings and gets none is itself reported, because a flag nobody checks is a flag that rots.

A FACT FROM ANOTHER PLANE. The fact shape is one shape on purpose (DN9b), so a verdict an org chart reached, or a count a notation picture keeps, can be quoted in a caption about a drawing of it.
