EXECUTION TRUST POSTURES (5.8) -------------------------------------- A posture says WHERE a refinement came from. Trust rank (high -> low): :trusted (3, in-process/verified) > :external (2, an external tool) > :sandboxed (1, isolated run) > :llm (0, LLM-composed).
