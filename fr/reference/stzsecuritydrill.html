Contain
Contain what the evidence shows, as poOperator. The plan is derived FROM THE ACQUIRED EVIDENCE (an incident per credential-stuffing finding), governed HERE -- MayCommit is judged on the operator, and an LLM actor is refused -- and carried to the target, whose REAL responder (stzAuthResponder over its own stzAuth) performs it. Then containment is VERIFIED from outside: the right password must now be refused, and the session opened before the attack must be dead. Returns the number of actions committed.
