Concurrence et distribution

D'excellents substrats : nœuds, supervision, multicœur, flotte ; le vocabulaire unificateur n'existe qu'en conception.

6 Strong 4 Solid 1 Partial 1 Emerging

Mesuré contre : Go · Akka · Erlang/OTP · Ray · Dossiers : base/concurrent(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet) · base/cluster(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet) · base/network(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet) · Pour les programmeurs : la référence de ce domaine

Où il en est

La lecture du Compas Softanza : chaque couloir du domaine noté face au meilleur de sa catégorie, d'après ce que les gardes prouvent (Read from the source on 2026-09-16, from a read-only main worktree). Les phrases du Compas sont en anglais, telles qu'il les a écrites.

An unusual shape: the substrates are complete and excellent, while the vocabulary over them is design-only. Three sub-planes ship, each heavily guarded with real multi-process tests — message-passing nodes with OTP-style supervision (the pragmatic 80% of Erlang: mailboxes, location-transparent Send/Ask, restart budgets, heartbeats, topology-as-data); a measured-gated multicore tier (bit-identical or justified, elementwise killed by the memory wall); and an elastic HTTP worker fleet with a smart router and autoscaling — over a full libuv reactor with mTLS. What's not built is the plane's own headline: the one "routing vocabulary" (Concurrently()) that would let you say "do this concurrently" and have it land on the right substrate.

The honesty line on the ratings. Re-surveyed from main. The substrates are guarded with real multi-process tests — D0–D6 spawn 2–6 OS processes, D3 performs real kills, R8 runs a 3-process fleet — proving FIFO-by-content, exact overflow counts, location-transparency by byte-identical results, restart-by-surviving-state and at-most-once. Two test-runner artifacts to note: the cluster _ERR_RUN.txt shows one error for a file that does not exist in the tree (a stale harness entry; the 20+ narrated guards aren't in that record), and the network _ERR_RUN.txt ran only 2 of ~26 suites. And base/concurrent/ is genuinely empty — the routing vocabulary does not exist yet.

The one-sentence position. Softanza has category-competitive concurrency substrates — Erlang-shaped supervised nodes, a measured multicore tier, an elastic worker fleet, a real async reactor — and lacks the ergonomic vocabulary that would let a user reach them without knowing which one they're using; the plane's own headline is its one big gap.

Ce qu'un artisan en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • OTP-style supervision proven under real kills -- the pragmatic 80% of Erlang in a non-BEAM language
  • A measured-gated multicore tier: bit-identical or justified, elementwise killed by the memory wall
  • A full libuv reactor where no callback crosses into Haro (submit -> ticket -> await)
  • An elastic HTTP worker fleet with a smart router and autoscaling, guarded with a real 3-process fleet

Ce qui est dû

  • The routing vocabulary: Concurrently() + stzTask/ticket/stzWorkshop (CN0-CN5)
  • Channels and structured concurrency
  • Federation over the node plane; UDP and a native WebSocket; a managed-PKI mTLS story
  • Dynamic task-graph distribution and live multi-machine verification

Repensé depuis les premiers principes

Ce que Softanza a gardé de ce qui marche, et ce qu'elle a repensé, tel que la bibliothèque l'a écrit dans ses documents de conception et ses narrations.

La manière SoftanzaLe processus est l'unité naturelle de la concurrence : les nœuds ne partagent rien, ne communiquent que par messages, et chaque passage a un coût mesuré qui décide s'il vaut la peine.

Gardé des meilleures pratiques

  • D'Erlang, Softanza reprend les processus sans partage, les messages comme seul contact, la transparence de l'emplacement et des superviseurs qui relancent les nœuds selon des stratégies déclarées. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La boucle d'événements est libuv, intégrée et compilée depuis les sources, plutôt qu'un réacteur maison : ne pas réinventer la roue, et profiter de son support Windows éprouvé. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La sécurité réutilise l'existant : le même signataire HMAC et le même canal TLS mutuel que le reste de la bibliothèque. Aucune cryptographie nouvelle, aucun second signataire. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

Repensé

  • La partie difficile est refusée par écrit : ni livraison exactement-une-fois, ni consensus, ni courtier. « Au plus une fois » est le défaut honnête ; l'état partagé va dans une base de données. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Aucune attente n'est infinie, aucune file n'est muette. Chaque requête porte un délai obligatoire, et chaque boîte aux lettres est bornée et compte ce qu'elle rejette. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Une topologie est une donnée qui se décrit et se recrée à l'identique. Déplacer un nœud sur une autre machine tient en une ligne : Deploy. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La concurrence est un vocabulaire d'aiguillage, pas une machine : Concurrently envoie le travail au moteur, à la boucle ou aux processus, et ne répartit que si la mesure le justifie. (prévu) source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

12 couloirs, chacun face au meilleur de sa catégorie

Pour chaque couloir : ce qui tourne dans le moteur aujourd'hui, les outils contre lesquels il est juste de le mesurer, et ce qui reste dû.

01Message-passing nodes & mailboxesStrong

In the engine

Share-nothing nodes (stzNode) with an engine-enforced bounded inbox (counted overflow: drop-oldest / drop-newest / refuse), On(tag,f) dispatch, and a registry (stzNodePlane) giving location-transparent Send (fire-forget) and Ask (reply, mandatory timeout) over an in-house STZM wire codec.

Measured against

Erlang / Elixir processesAkkaGo channels

To complete

Guarded with real OS processes (D0–D2): FIFO-by-content, exact overflow counts, byte-identical results local vs remote. Genuinely Erlang-shaped for a non-BEAM language.

02Actor supervisionStrong

In the engine

stzNodeSupervisor: :OneForOne / :AllForOne strategies, a restart budget (max/window) with escalation, heartbeats that catch a wedged node, and death-notice monitors — pure policy over engine observations, hostable on an agent host.

Measured against

Erlang/OTP supervisorsAkka

To complete

Proven by surviving vs wiped state under real kills (D3): OneForOne preserves a sibling, AllForOne resets it, budget escalates, heartbeats catch the wedge. OTP-grade supervision, guarded.

03Distributed computing (D0–D6)Strong

In the engine

Share-nothing OS processes exchanging STZM frames over reactor TCP/mTLS, addressed by name, supervised. Topology as data (Describe() emits plain nested lists; handlers are Haro code strings so they cross a process boundary); Deploy moves a node in one line.

Measured against

Erlang distributionAkka ClusterRay

To complete

Complete and guarded end-to-end (D4–D6, a semantic-search node Deploy'd and killed mid-run). It is process-pool + message-passing distribution — the pragmatic 80% of Erlang — not Ray-class dynamic task graphs (lane 11).

04Multicore parallel tier (M1–M5)Strong

In the engine

Five parallel sites, each a std.Thread.spawn behind a measured calib.Gate (failed spawn computes inline): row-banded matmul, gated-parallel LU, compensated threaded reductions, and kNN / distance scans — 2.7–6.7×, transparent (the user never asks).

Measured against

Rayon (Rust)OpenMPGo parallelism

To complete

Correctness is two-class: partition-output is bit-identical (asserted), reduce-across-threads carries a written justification. Elementwise was killed — the memory wall (one core already pulls ~39 of ~58 GB/s).

05Async reactor / event loopStrong

In the engine

reactor.zig — vendored libuv on its own thread, a submit→ticket→await idiom where no callback crosses into Haro: async timers, TCP, subprocess (with kill), curl HTTPS, and HTTP/STZM/TLS listeners, plus a reactor pool of N loops.

Measured against

libuv / NodeGo netpollerTokio

To complete

A genuine async runtime with a clean no-VM-callback model and mTLS termination. The ergonomic surface over it (channels, structured concurrency) is the vocabulary gap (lane 12).

06Worker fleet & autoscaling (R8)Strong

In the engine

An elastic HTTP fleet: stzWorkerPool (admission / load-isolation), a 3-tier stzRequestClassifier (rules / lexical / model), stzAppCluster (front host + worker processes via reactor spawn+curl), stzClusterSupervisor (autoscale on live /health), staged stzComputePipeline, and p50–p99 telemetry.

Measured against

Ray ServeCeleryKubernetes + workers

To complete

Guarded with a real 3-process fleet (health-wait, round-robin, teardown). A Ray-Serve-shaped fleet for HTTP facets — the reach is live multi-machine verification (Tier C).

07Fault tolerance & hardeningSolid

In the engine

Retry-with-failover + per-worker circuit breakers, token-bucket rate limiting, at-most-once delivery (late replies discarded by correlation id), mandatory timeouts, Drain/Stop — with a hardening guard against SSRF, CRLF smuggling and injection.

Measured against

Akka / OTP resilienceHystrixEnvoy

To complete

A real resilience toolkit, guarded — but fleet-scoped; the general per-call policy vocabulary (bulkheads, adaptive concurrency) is thinner than a dedicated service mesh.

08Networking (HTTP / TCP)Solid

In the engine

HTTP client (sync http.zig + async reactor curl), TCP client/server (blocking tcp.zig + async reactor TCP), URL parsing and DNS — HTTP framing fuzzed and property-tested.

Measured against

Go net/httpRust hyper / reqwestNode

To complete

No UDP, WebSocket is still curl-based (not a native implementation), and the plain client/server faces are blocking. HTTP/TCP are covered; the edges are not.

09Transport security (mTLS)Solid

In the engine

Mutual TLS via vendored mbedTLS in the reactor: server termination with require_client, a client role (reactor_connect_tls, link-up only on completed handshake), and a reactor-free smoke guard (mtls_smoke.zig, exit-code gated).

Measured against

Go crypto/tlsrustlsErlang ssl

To complete

Real mutual auth, but test certs only — no cert lifecycle, rotation, OCSP or real-CA verification story. It proves the handshake, not a managed PKI.

10Compute federationSolid

In the engine

stzComputeFederation: a governed multi-host constellation — Join(host,endpoint,facets), a governance lattice (risk/grant/authority), and FederatedCall (discovered → governed → transported over reactor async curl).

Measured against

Ray clusterDask distributedSpark

To complete

Unified at the vocabulary level (one signer, one lattice, one TLS channel) but facet discovery is still HTTP, not re-expressed over the node plane (D5 residue) — and it is governed remote-call, not a distributed scheduler.

11Dynamic task-graph distributionPartial

In the engine

Distribution today routes named work — messages to nodes, requests to facet workers. Arbitrary task submission with data-dependency scheduling is not built.

Measured against

Ray (@remote / task graph)DaskSpark DAG

To complete

No Ray-style dynamic task graph — submit an arbitrary function, let a scheduler place it and track its dependencies. The plan explicitly refuses this until a workload earns it; message-passing is the deliberate model.

12The routing vocabulary (Concurrently)Emerging

In the engine

A detailed plan of record (CN0–CN5): one adverb Concurrently(), a stzTask / ticket / stzWorkshop surface that routes work to the right substrate (engine threads / reactor / processes) by declaration + measurement, never guessed. No code exists — base/concurrent/ holds only the plan; its test dir is empty.

Measured against

Go (go + channels)Ray (@remote)async/await

To complete

The plane's headline is unbuilt: the three substrates it would route over all ship, but the adverb, task, ticket and workshop that make concurrency ergonomic — and a channel/select primitive — do not.

Ce qui est dû, dans l'ordre du rendement

Les manques, classés par ce qu'ils rapportent et non par leur taille ; la valeur est l'effet sur la portée de la bibliothèque, l'effort une lecture d'ingénieur.

Tier A — the ergonomic layer the substrates deserve

Build the routing vocabulary (CN0–CN5)

Concurrently() + stzTask / ticket / stzWorkshop that route to engine threads, the reactor, or processes by declaration and measured break-even. Three strong substrates exist; this is the single move that makes them one usable concurrency model.

Valeur · élevéeEffort · élevé
Channels & structured concurrency

A first-class channel / select primitive and scoped task groups over the mailbox + reactor substrates — the Go/structured-concurrency ergonomics that make fan-out and coordination readable rather than hand-wired.

Valeur · élevéeEffort · moyen

Tier B — transport & networking edges

Federation over the node plane

Re-express stzComputeFederation discovery/transport over the node registry (the D5 residue), so distribution is one message plane rather than HTTP-discovery plus node-plane split.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen
UDP & a native WebSocket

A UDP surface and a first-party WebSocket (today it rides curl CONNECT_ONLY) — the two networking edges the HTTP/TCP core leaves open.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen
A managed-PKI mTLS story

Beyond test certs and a handshake: cert lifecycle, rotation, real-CA verification and OCSP — so mutual TLS is deployable, not just demonstrable.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen

Tier C — scheduler reach

Dynamic task-graph distribution

Ray-style arbitrary-task submission with dependency scheduling across the fleet — deliberately refused today. Strategic if a workload needs a distributed scheduler rather than named message-passing.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · élevé
Live multi-machine verification

The fleet and federation are proven on local processes; running and verifying them across real machines (and the deploy plane's ssh/registry backends with them) makes "distributed" true in fact, not just in loopback.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen
The refused-until-measured multicore sites

Parallel Cholesky / QR / back-substitution — held back because their reductions sit under the oracle tolerances. Admit them once a measurement makes the correctness case, as the tier's own discipline requires.

Valeur · faibleEffort · moyen
