Palier neuronal

Un vrai moteur d'inférence, vérifié contre numpy (BERT et LLM, décodage contraint) ; l'entraînement et la fonderie sont dus.

4 S4 So3 P1 E

Mesuré contre: ggml/llama.cpp · ONNX Runtime · Dossiers: base/neural

The consumption side is complete and genuinely wired to a patched, vendored ggml: real GGUF loading, id-exact tokenizers, a numpy-parity BERT encoder, a multi-architecture causal LLM decoder with grammar-constrained decoding, and a measurement-gated GPU path. What is not there is the production side. A llama.cpp-class runner, wired into a governed library — not yet a training or model-production platform.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • numpy-parity BERT encoder (1.64e-7)
  • grammar-constrained decoding wired to the structured-output contract
  • id-exact WordPiece and BPE tokenizers
  • measurement-gated wgpu GPU path (2-5x)

Ce qui est dû

  • wire ggml's backward pass -- the training foundry
  • a quantization (encode) path
  • ship or fetch a real semantic model for CI
  • ONNX import

GGUF model loading
Strong
neural_embed.zig loads GGUF metadata + weights into a ggml context; F16/Q8_0 de-quant on load; tested against a committed tiny_bert.ggufneural_embed.zig; tiny_bert.gguf
Tokenizers
Strong
WordPiece/SentencePiece-metaspace and GPT-2 byte-level BPE from the GGUF vocab; tokenization is id-exact against a numpy reference
Encoder inference (BERT family)
Strong
Full BERT-family forward (MiniLM/BERT/RoBERTa/jina-bert-v2): embeddings, NER, rerank, matching an independent numpy reference to 1.64e-7neural_embed.zig; test/neural BERT numpy-parity 1.64e-7
Constrained / structured decoding
Strong
GBNF grammar-constrained decoding makes invalid tokens unemittable; ~5 calls per valid answer without the grammar, one withgbnf_machine.zig / schema_gbnf.zig; test/neural GBNF
Decoder inference (causal LLM)
Solid
llama/qwen2/qwen3/smollm with KV-cache, sampling, streaming; CI has no generative model, so proven by construction, not by a parity fixtureneural_gen.zig
Tensor ops & the numeric bridge
Solid
Vendored ggml tensor core wired; stzMatrix<->ggml bridge routes f64 matmul through ggml; the bridge is matmul-only, no backward passneural_matmul.zig
Semantic index & embeddings pipeline
Solid
stzSemanticIndex AddText/Search over StzNeuralEmbeddingOf; shipped and tested, but the strong model is user-supplied (only tiny_bert.gguf tracked)stzSemanticIndex
GPU execution
Solid
wgpu resident BERT backbone auto-routes to GPU via the calibration foundry: 2.08x on the backbone forward, 5.3x over full CPU forward; training sees no GPUneural_backbone.zig (GK0-GK2c)
Training & backward pass
Partial
nn.zig forward + backward SGD for small dense MLPs (learns XOR, tested); no transformer training, ggml-opt.cpp vendored but never callednn.zig; test/learning XOR training
Quantization
Partial
Consume-only: F16 and Q8_0 de-quantized on load (dequantRows); no encode path, GGUF export writes F32 only
GGUF export & the foundry
Partial
gguf_export.zig writes GGUF for small F32 nets (stz-mlp, bigram DLM), round-trip verified; foundry rung 2 (train a transformer) is unbuiltgguf_export.zig
ONNX interop
Emerging
None: GGUF is the chosen and only model format; no ONNX import, runtime or export anywhere in the engine or faces
