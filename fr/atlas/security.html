Sécurité et gouvernance

Une doctrine de gouvernance, un modèle ne peut pas agir par construction, plus l'identité, un registre durable inviolable et une réponse mesurée.

6 S5 So1 E

Mesuré contre: OWASP · Vault · OPA · semgrep · Dossiers: base/security · La page d'origine de l'Atlas

Most security stacks are a bag of primitives; Softanza's is a doctrine. One capability lattice (effectful, sensing, compute, inference) and three postures gate every dangerous act uniformly, so an LLM actor holding inference only is refused a secret, a file write or a deployment by construction. On top sit a full identity stack (OIDC, SAML, WebAuthn, both sides) and an engine-computed tamper-evident audit ledger; the gaps are a unified injection-defense surface, the unbuilt plugin governance, and the absence of a formal threat-model document.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • A governance doctrine enforced uniformly: an LLM actor holding only inference is refused by construction, not by a rule someone remembered to write
  • An engine-computed tamper-evident audit ledger (hash-chained, seal and verify)
  • A full identity stack: OIDC, SAML and WebAuthn on both sides
  • Multi-hop escalation caught by graph reachability (ReachesEffectful, BlastRadius)

Ce qui est dû

  • A unified injection-defense surface (the OWASP Top-10 core)
  • Authenticated symmetric encryption (AES-GCM / ChaCha20-Poly1305) and a modern KDF (Argon2)
  • A formal threat model mapped to guards
  • Plugin / foreign-capability governance, plus a shipped Vault/KMS backend adapter

Tamper-evident audit ledger
Strong
stzSecurityLedger over seclog.zig: a bounded hash-chained circular buffer whose digest is computed in-engine; seal-to-file, verify names a broken linkengine/src/seclog.zig
Cryptographic primitives
Solid
crypto.zig ships SHA-256, HMAC, PBKDF2, constant-time compare, CSPRNG, TOTP, ES256/RS256; RSA/X.509 via vendored mbedTLS; no AEAD, no Argon2engine crypto.zig + vendored mbedTLS
Secret storage & keyring
Solid
stzSecretStore is the one governed door: every Reveal(name, actor) is gated and audited, secrets self-redact; no real backend adapter shipsstzVaultResolver (in-memory reference only)
Policy governance & security rules
Solid
stzSecurityGraph answers ReachesEffectful, BlastRadius and PathExists, catching multi-hop escalation; only three security rules shipstzSecurityRule -> stzRuleReport
Events, redaction & OCSF telemetry
Solid
stzSecurityEvent is a typed record with a MITRE ATT&CK field and OCSF JSON export; redaction is enforced at construction; no PII/DLP surface
Detection & incident response
Solid
stzDetection -> stzSecuritySentinel -> stzIncident -> stzResponsePlan -> stzSecurityDrill; three detection shapes, no anomaly/ML, no Sigma interopstzSecurityDrill (adversary emulation vs a real spawned app)
Attestation & evidence custody
Solid
stzSecurityAttestation makes a custody statement over a sealed ledger; export is a sensing act an LLM may not perform; no in-toto interop yet
TLS / transport security
Partial
mTLS and TLS ride vendored mbedTLS with a smoke guard and fuzz_tls.zig; no cert-lifecycle management, OCSP or cipher-policy governancetest/cluster/mtls_narrated; engine fuzz_tls.zig
Input validation & injection defense
Partial
Primitives exist scattered (HTML/URL escaping in encode.zig, XXE closed by the XML parser); no unified validation surface, no SQLi/CSRF/XSS toolkitengine encode.zig
Plugin / foreign-capability governance
Emerging
A designed doctrine ('a plugin is a governed foreign capability') with a plan and comparative study; base/plugin is not loaded in stzBaseSOFTANZA_PLUGIN_PLAN.md
