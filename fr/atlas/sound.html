Son

Entièrement livré : un rappel sans verrou, un graphe possédé, une analyse de niveau librosa, la sonification.

5 Strong · 5 Solid · 1 Partial · 1 Emerging

Mesuré contre : miniaudio · Web Audio · JUCE · Dossiers : base/sound(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet) · Le guide des fonctions de ce domaine

Repensé depuis les premiers principes

Ce que Softanza a gardé de ce qui marche, et ce qu'elle a repensé, tel que la bibliothèque l'a écrit dans ses documents de conception et ses narrations.

La manière SoftanzaUn son se décrit, il ne se fabrique pas à la main : on déclare un graphe ou un sens, le moteur le produit, et une oreille humaine juge en dernier.

Gardé des meilleures pratiques

  • Les entrées-sorties audio et le décodage de fichiers viennent de miniaudio : un seul fichier du domaine public, sans dépendance, pour les trois systèmes. Seule la moitié manquante est empruntée. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La règle du temps réel est stricte : le rappel audio n'alloue pas, ne verrouille pas et n'appelle pas le langage de la plateforme ; il lit des tampons déjà prêts. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La musique reprend l'algèbre de partition d'Euterpea : notes et silences enchaînés ou superposés, répétés, transposés. Une partition est une donnée qu'on peut analyser avant de l'entendre. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

Repensé

  • Un même graphe sonore joue sur le haut-parleur ou s'écrit dans un fichier par le même chemin : ce qu'on entend et ce qu'on exporte ne peuvent pas diverger. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Un auteur déclenche un sens, comme « danger », et obtient un son. Une alerte qu'on peut couvrir n'en est pas une : les sons moins importants baissent ou se taisent. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Un univers culturel se déclare comme des données : accordage, règles de mouvement, cycle rythmique, ornements et instruments ; une même phrase peut alors sonner en maqam, en raga ou en gamelan. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • L'oreille est le dernier juge : une voix lue deux fois trop vite a passé tous les compteurs et c'est un auditeur qui l'a repérée. Chaque phase note qui a écouté. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

The survey's biggest surprise: memory said 'plan, SN0 next', the code says one of the most fully-realized planes in the library, SN0-SN6 and a sonification series shipped and closed. miniaudio is vendored and split into two DLLs, there is an owned Zig sound graph (Web-Audio-shaped), FFT-backed analysis, and a real lock-free real-time callback that does no allocation, no lock, and never re-enters the Haro VM. What it refuses on purpose: MIDI, spatial/HRTF, plugin hosting, notation; and reverb is absent even from the effect palette.

Ce qu'un artisan en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • A lock-free real-time callback: no alloc, no lock, no Haro re-entry, guarded down to worst-callback microseconds
  • An owned Zig sound graph, Web-Audio-shaped, not a wrapper
  • librosa-grade analysis: spectrogram, onsets, tempo and LUFS on the shared engine FFT
  • A sonification / earcon / voice layer no mainstream audio library offers

Ce qui est dû

  • Reverb and a fuller effect palette (compressor, chorus, distortion)
  • Richer synthesis (wavetable, FM, additive, granular)
  • MP3 / OGG encode to close the read-broad / write-narrow asymmetry
  • MIDI input, spatial/HRTF and plugin hosting (deliberate non-goals)

La manière Softanza, exécutée

Ce que Softanza fait autrement dans ce domaine, montré par du code tiré de ses narrations et de ses gardes, et exécuté pour cette page.

Pendant qu'une alerte de danger retentit, un signal de succès et un avertissement sont abandonnés ; ensuite tout repasse, et un avertissement ne fait jamais taire le danger.

Softanza
oP = new stzEarcons()
oP.RecordFireAt("Danger.Alert", 10.0)
Chk("a cue under a sounding alert is DROPPED", NOT oP.WouldFireAt(:Success, 10.05))
Chk("and so is a warning -- rank, not recency", NOT oP.WouldFireAt(:Warning, 10.05))
Chk("once the alert is over, a cue passes again", oP.WouldFireAt(:Success, 12.0))
oP2 = new stzEarcons()
oP2.RecordFireAt("Warning.Alert", 10.0)
Chk("a warning alert does NOT pre-empt danger", oP2.WouldFireAt(:Danger, 10.05))

Les fonctions, par ce qu'elles font

Tester et vérifier

  • IsSilentValue Muted is the one value whose rendering is nothing. Asking is legitimate. dans stzEarcons
  • CanAcknowledgeWithin Rule 18 allows 100 ms. This answers whether a sound can be the acknowledgement, and on this pipeline the answer is no. dans stzEarcons
  • IsRunning what happened dans stzSoundLive
  • Check The movement rules this mode declares, held against a phrase: a list of what breaks them, each as text. An empty list is a phrase the declaration permits -- not a phrase a musician would call right. dans stzSoundUniverse
  • IsUsable can this machine speak at all? -------------------------------------- ASK THIS FIRST. A machine with no speech engine is a supported configuration, not a broken install, and every other path still works. dans stzVoice
  • IsStarted what it is doing dans stzVoicePool
  • IsAvailable readers ------------------------------------------------------------- Is there anything to record WITH? A machine with no input, or no device DLL at all, is a supported configuration -- CI is one. dans stzMicrophone
  • IsAllStrokes True when every word is a stroke: the pattern names its own drum. dans stzSoundPattern

Trouver

  • PositionInFrames Frames the DEVICE has consumed, not frames the graph has produced: the producer runs ahead by a ring's worth, so asking it would report a position up to a third of a second in the future. What the listener has heard is what the device has taken. dans stzSoundTransport

Remplacer et modifier

  • SetDuckDepth SS3: ducking -------------------------------------------------------- How deep, in dB. Declared, never inferred -- a caller who wants a different depth argues with a number. dans stzEarcons
  • SetAmbientFloor Colour's doctrine, transposed: a sound system that cannot fail an audibility check does not have one. The check is a MARGIN over the floor. dans stzEarcons
  • SetVoiceLanguage VC4: SAY -- the earcon, then the phrase that says WHICH ------------- Which language the phrase is spoken in. REFUSED rather than substituted if the machine has no voice for it -- speaking French to an operator who asked for English is worse than saying nothing. dans stzEarcons
  • SetTempo settings dans stzSoundLive
  • SetLength The length in beats, set outright -- a pattern's cycles end where they end, whatever their last note does. dans stzSoundScore
  • SetVelocity The velocity of notes ADDED FROM NOW ON, 0 < v <= 1. dans stzSoundScore
  • SetOutputTo output -------------------------------------------------------------- Which node the graph hands to the world. Defaults to the last thing added, which is right far more often than not. dans stzSoundGraph
  • SetRate SAPI takes rate -10..10 and volume 0..100 and IGNORES anything else. The engine clamps and reports, because a setting that silently does nothing is worse than one that is visibly limited. dans stzVoice
  • SetLookahead settings ------------------------------------------------------------ Frames of lookahead. Set it small only to PROVE the engine counts lateness -- the negative sibling in the guard does exactly that. dans stzSoundScheduler
  • SetVoiceGain The write is a single atomic store on a node the render already reads every block. Nothing is allocated, nothing is rebuilt, and the audio thread never learns that a duck happened. dans stzVoicePool
  • SetNote One line under the plot saying what the picture MEANS. A chart that needs a paragraph of explanation elsewhere is a chart that failed. dans stzSoundPlot

Ajouter

  • AddNotes Space-separated names, one beat each; the octave carries; ~ rests. dans stzSoundScore
  • AddMixOf mixing -------------------------------------------------------------- Mix several named nodes into one. The engine requires every input to EXIST before the mix does -- that is what makes a cycle impossible -- so this verb takes them all at once rather than letting you add to a mix later and discover the refusal. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddSound Play an existing sound (a stzSound, or an engine buffer id) as a source. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddEchoOn ECHO, not "reverb". SN4's challenge pass refused to call a delay a reverb: one repeat fading out is not a diffuse room, and a name that promises a hall and delivers a slapback survives into documentation. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddGainOn the shapers: each takes WHAT it acts on, by name dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddTimeline MU2: a node whose output is the notes placed on it, each at the frame asked -- inside a block, not at its top. It has no input. Returns the node (1-based), which StzEngineSoundGraphTimelinePlace takes. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddMasterGain This is what a transport fades on pause. It exists as a verb here rather than in the transport because only the graph knows what its output IS -- and putting one gain at the end means a pause is one click-free ramp instead of a walk over every voice, whatever the caller built upstream. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddOscillator the sources --------------------------------------------------------- WAVEFORM BY NAME, not by number: :Sine :Triangle :Square :Saw. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddEnvelopeOnAt The same envelope, starting nStart seconds in. THIS is what makes a score possible: a voice that knows WHEN it sounds is a note. dans stzSoundGraph
  • AddVoice building it --------------------------------------------------------- A voice made from an existing sound. pnSlots is how many of it may sound at the same time. dans stzVoicePool
  • AddToneVoice A voice made from an oscillator and an envelope -- a blip, a coin, a beep. pnSeconds is how long one shot lasts. dans stzVoicePool

Convertir

  • ToSoundOf what a meaning sounds like (no device needed) ----------------------- The motif as DATA. Works with no audio hardware, which is what lets a CI machine assert the vocabulary. dans stzEarcons
  • ToSoundOfSaying Needs no audio device, which is what lets a guard measure the composition on a machine that cannot play it. dans stzEarcons
  • ToList The samples of one channel, as a Haro list. DATA, so To... is right. Be aware this crosses the FFI once per sample -- fine for a look at a few thousand frames, wrong for a whole song. dans stzSound
  • ToOnsets Where notes START, in seconds -- a one-row grid of times. dans stzSound
  • ToSpectrum To...() because these RETURN things. A spectrum is a stzSoundGrid: rows by columns of numbers, with x_step and y_step saying what a row and a column MEAN. Drawing one is the graphics plane's job, and keeping that boundary is what makes the analysis assertable -- "the 1 kHz sine is in the 1 kHz bin" is a claim about numbers; "the picture looks right" is a claim about nothing. The magnitude spectrum of one window. Default 4096 points: about 12 Hz resolution at 48 kHz, which resolves musical pitch without smearing time. dans stzSound
  • ToWavBytes The symmetric half of loading from memory, and its absence was a real gap: a sound could be decoded FROM bytes but not encoded back TO them, so handing a buffer to another tier meant a temporary file. VC1 went to some trouble to keep a voice out of the filesystem; without this, VC3 would have put it straight back to feed a recogniser. dans stzSound
  • ToSpectrogram Frequency against time: one row per window. MULTICORE by default -- every row is an independent FFT over a different slice of the same immutable buffer, which is the one analysis here that parallelises without an argument. dans stzSound
  • ToSound performing it ------------------------------------------------------- The whole performance, rendered offline into one sound. Every note is the instrument's (MU1), placed at FrameOf() by mixInto. dans stzSoundScore
  • ToVoiceList The whole capability matrix for this direction: [ [name, tag], ... ] dans stzVoice
  • ToSoundOfSsml The same, with SSML prosody. The engine validates the markup, because the platform does not: handed an unclosed tag SAPI silently discards it and speaks the words, so a typo means the prosody never happens and nobody is told. dans stzVoice
  • ToTransportOf never left it: the phrase ended, the transport kept rendering silence, and IsPlaying stayed true forever. A spoken phrase has a KNOWN length, so the transport is given it. The trap was real enough to hang this face's own demo before it was found. dans stzVoice
  • ToPNG PNG goes through the GPU tier, so it needs a device. SVG does not. dans stzSoundGrid

Afficher, dessiner et mettre en forme

  • RenderThroughRing The SAME real-time path -- a producer thread rendering the graph into a ring, this thread posting notes ahead of it -- with this thread also draining the ring instead of a sound card. It runs as fast as the machine can, which is HARDER for the scheduler than a device: the producer is never waiting for the speaker. What comes out is returned as a sound, so a guard can hold it against the offline render sample for sample. dans stzSoundScheduler
  • DrawWave THE WAVEFORM dans stzSoundPlot
  • DrawSpectra THE SPECTRUM (one or more series) ----------------------------------- paSeries: [ [cLabel, oGrid], ... ] -- each a one-row spectrum grid. Log frequency across, decibels up. Several series is a CATEGORICAL job: fixed hue order, a legend always, and a direct label on each line. dans stzSoundPlot
  • DrawSpectrogram THE SPECTROGRAM ----------------------------------------------------- Time across, frequency up, magnitude as lightness. nMaxHz crops the top: nearly all musical energy lives low, and drawing to Nyquist spends most of the picture on silence. dans stzSoundPlot

Parcourir et appliquer

  • PerformQ performing a phrase --------------------------------------------------- The phrase, once per cycle for `pnCycles` cycles, in this mode, over this cycle, with its ornaments and accents, as a stzSoundScore. One phrase cycle fills one rhythmic cycle, however many beats that has. dans stzSoundUniverse
  • RunToEnd Tick until it stops, without a reactive loop. This DOES occupy the thread -- it is the honest version of PlayFor for a script that has nothing else to do, and it still reports the position while it runs. dans stzSoundTransport
