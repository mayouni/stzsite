Son

Entièrement livré : un rappel sans verrou, un graphe possédé, une analyse de niveau librosa, la sonification.

Où il en est

La lecture du Compas Softanza : chaque couloir du domaine noté face au meilleur de sa catégorie, d'après ce que les gardes prouvent (Read from the source on 2026-09-16, from a read-only main worktree -- base/sound; engine sound.zig, soundgraph.zig, soundanalysis.zig, audiodev.zig, sounddsp.zig, soundring.zig, soundwasm.zig + vendored miniaudio 0.11.25; guards under test/sound (31 files + a web-audio harness)). Les phrases du Compas sont en anglais, telles qu'il les a écrites.

The survey's biggest surprise. Memory said "plan, SN0 next" — the code says one of the most fully-realized planes in the library: SN0–SN6 and a sonification series shipped and closed. miniaudio is vendored and split into two DLLs, there's an owned Zig sound graph (Web-Audio-shaped), FFT-backed analysis on the shared engine FFT, and — the pro-audio correctness axis JUCE obsesses over — a real lock-free real-time callback that does no allocation, no lock, and never re-enters the Haro VM, guarded down to worst-callback microseconds. On top sits a distinctive sonification / earcon / voice layer no mainstream audio library offers. What it refuses on purpose: MIDI, spatial/HRTF, plugin hosting, notation — and reverb is absent even from the effect palette.

The honesty line on the ratings — and a memory correction. Re-surveyed from main, and the project-memory line ("Sound plane PLAN, SN0 next") is stale: SN0 was the go/no-go spike and everything through SN6 plus a sonification series SS1–SS5 shipped and closed. It is guarded by a 31-file test corpus plus a WASM / Web-Audio browser guard harness (earcon_expect.json, voice_guard.html), and the real-time callback contract (no alloc / no lock / no Haro) is implemented and guarded — SPSC ring with magic/version checks and worst-callback-microsecond tracking. The deliberate non-goals (MIDI, notation, 3D/HRTF, time-stretch, VST/CLAP) are stated in writing; reverb is absent as a named effect.

The one-sentence position. Softanza's sound is a real, correctly-built audio engine — a lock-free realtime callback, an owned Web-Audio-shaped graph, librosa-grade analysis, and a sonification layer no competitor has — that stops short of a pro DAW/host on purpose: a thin effect palette (no reverb) and the deliberate absence of MIDI, spatial audio and plugin hosting.

Ce qu'un artisan en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • A lock-free real-time callback: no alloc, no lock, no Haro re-entry, guarded down to worst-callback microseconds
  • An owned Zig sound graph, Web-Audio-shaped, not a wrapper
  • librosa-grade analysis: spectrogram, onsets, tempo and LUFS on the shared engine FFT
  • A sonification / earcon / voice layer no mainstream audio library offers

Ce qui est dû

  • Reverb and a fuller effect palette (compressor, chorus, distortion)
  • Richer synthesis (wavetable, FM, additive, granular)
  • MP3 / OGG encode to close the read-broad / write-narrow asymmetry
  • MIDI input, spatial/HRTF and plugin hosting (deliberate non-goals)

Repensé depuis les premiers principes

Ce que Softanza a gardé de ce qui marche, et ce qu'elle a repensé, tel que la bibliothèque l'a écrit dans ses documents de conception et ses narrations.

La manière SoftanzaUn son se décrit, il ne se fabrique pas à la main : on déclare un graphe ou un sens, le moteur le produit, et une oreille humaine juge en dernier.

Gardé des meilleures pratiques

  • Les entrées-sorties audio et le décodage de fichiers viennent de miniaudio : un seul fichier du domaine public, sans dépendance, pour les trois systèmes. Seule la moitié manquante est empruntée. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La règle du temps réel est stricte : le rappel audio n'alloue pas, ne verrouille pas et n'appelle pas le langage de la plateforme ; il lit des tampons déjà prêts. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • La musique reprend l'algèbre de partition d'Euterpea : notes et silences enchaînés ou superposés, répétés, transposés. Une partition est une donnée qu'on peut analyser avant de l'entendre. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

Repensé

  • Un même graphe sonore joue sur le haut-parleur ou s'écrit dans un fichier par le même chemin : ce qu'on entend et ce qu'on exporte ne peuvent pas diverger. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Un auteur déclenche un sens, comme « danger », et obtient un son. Une alerte qu'on peut couvrir n'en est pas une : les sons moins importants baissent ou se taisent. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • Un univers culturel se déclare comme des données : accordage, règles de mouvement, cycle rythmique, ornements et instruments ; une même phrase peut alors sonner en maqam, en raga ou en gamelan. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)
  • L'oreille est le dernier juge : une voix lue deux fois trop vite a passé tous les compteurs et c'est un auditeur qui l'a repérée. Chaque phase note qui a écouté. source(s'ouvre dans un nouvel onglet)

La manière Softanza, exécutée

Ce que Softanza fait autrement dans ce domaine, montré par du code tiré de ses narrations et de ses gardes, et exécuté pour cette page.

Pendant qu'une alerte de danger retentit, un signal de succès et un avertissement sont abandonnés ; ensuite tout repasse, et un avertissement ne fait jamais taire le danger.

Softanza
oP = new stzEarcons()
oP.RecordFireAt("Danger.Alert", 10.0)
Chk("a cue under a sounding alert is DROPPED", NOT oP.WouldFireAt(:Success, 10.05))
Chk("and so is a warning -- rank, not recency", NOT oP.WouldFireAt(:Warning, 10.05))
Chk("once the alert is over, a cue passes again", oP.WouldFireAt(:Success, 12.0))
oP2 = new stzEarcons()
oP2.RecordFireAt("Warning.Alert", 10.0)
Chk("a warning alert does NOT pre-empt danger", oP2.WouldFireAt(:Danger, 10.05))

12 couloirs, chacun face au meilleur de sa catégorie

Pour chaque couloir : ce qui tourne dans le moteur aujourd'hui, les outils contre lesquels il est juste de le mesurer, et ce qui reste dû.

01Real-time callback disciplineStrong

In the engine

The pro-audio correctness axis, implemented in Zig (audiodev.zig sinkCallback): the audio callback does no allocation, no lock, and never re-enters the Haro VM — an SPSC lock-free ring (soundring.zig, magic/version-checked, two atomics), capacity-reserved tables that never reallocate under a live callback, underruns counted, worst-callback microseconds tracked.

Measured against

JUCEminiaudioWeb Audio (AudioWorklet)

To complete

This is the discipline serious audio frameworks are built on, and it is genuinely built and guarded — not aspired to. A real strength.

02Device playback & transportStrong

In the engine

audiodev.zig over vendored miniaudio device backends (per-OS): open / start / stop / close, a stzSoundTransport face, and an honest report when no device is present. Split into a portable decode DLL and a device DLL.

Measured against

miniaudioPortAudioWeb Audio

To complete

Real cross-platform device I/O via the reference low-level library, wired with the correct callback discipline (lane 1). At parity for playback.

03Sound graph — mixing & routingStrong

In the engine

An owned node graph (soundgraph.zig — miniaudio's own graph is deliberately disabled): node kinds OSC / SOURCE / GAIN / MIX / PAN / FILTER / DELAY / ENVELOPE over planar buses, topological and cycle-free, with real-time streaming (graph + ring + producer thread).

Measured against

Web Audio AudioNode graphJUCE AudioProcessorGraphPure Data

To complete

The Web-Audio / JUCE graph model, built in-house — a real routing engine, not a wrapper. The node palette is where the effects gap sits (lane 11).

04Spectral analysisStrong

In the engine

soundanalysis.zig on the shared fft.zig: spectrum (Hann-windowed), threaded spectrogram, onset detection, tempo estimation, LUFS loudness (momentary + short-term, broadcast standard), and dominant frequency.

Measured against

Web Audio AnalyserNodelibrosaEssentia

To complete

Well beyond Web Audio's basic analyser — spectrogram, onsets, tempo and LUFS reach toward librosa territory. A genuinely rich analysis lane.

05Sonification, earcons & voiceStrong

In the engine

A distinctive shipped layer: stzEarcons (66 methods), stzVoice / stzVoicePool (text→sound), stzListener (sound→text, closed grammar), and the SS1–SS5 sonification series (contour/timbre identity, loudness binding, ducking, a shared data-to-sound vocabulary).

Measured against

(sonification libraries)earcon systemsWeb Audio (raw)

To complete

Turning data into meaningful sound with a governed vocabulary and ducking is something no mainstream audio library offers — a distinctive, shipped capability, consistent with the library's accessibility DNA.

06File decode / encodeSolid

In the engine

sound.zig decodes WAV / MP3 / FLAC / OGG (via miniaudio), with resample and channel conversion; encodes WAV and FLAC (SaveWav / SaveFlac / ToWavBytes).

Measured against

miniaudiolibsndfileWeb Audio decodeAudioData

To complete

Decode covers the common formats; encode is WAV/FLAC only — MP3/OGG out return UNSUPPORTED. Read-broad, write-narrow.

07SynthesisSolid

In the engine

sounddsp.zig — band-limited oscillators (polyBLEP / polyBLAMP anti-aliasing) for sine / square / saw / triangle, plus an ADSR Envelope, all f64 and unit-tested.

Measured against

Web Audio OscillatorNodeJUCE dspSuperCollider

To complete

Correct anti-aliased oscillators + ADSR — but a modest palette: no wavetable, FM, additive or granular synthesis, and one envelope shape.

08Capture / recordingSolid

In the engine

Microphone capture through the same device layer and the same callback discipline (audiodev.zig capture side, stzMicrophone.ring), reporting honestly when no input device is present.

Measured against

miniaudio captureWeb Audio getUserMediaJUCE

To complete

Real, lock-free mic capture — solid for recording and live input. Multi-device selection and format negotiation depth are where it stays basic.

09StreamingSolid

In the engine

Real-time streaming through the graph (soundgraph.zig "the stream"): a producer thread feeds the lock-free ring the callback drains, bounded at MAX_STREAMS=16, with StreamStart / Stop / Drain.

Measured against

Web Audio streamingJUCEminiaudio

To complete

Streaming from a producer to the device works and is bounded. Network streaming / adaptive buffering is not part of the lane.

10Audio↔graphics convergenceSolid

In the engine

stzSoundPlot and stzSoundGrid render waveforms and spectrograms through the library's own graphics plane — audio analysis drawn as SVG/PNG grids, plus a browser guard harness.

Measured against

Web Audio + canvasJUCE visualizerslibrosa.display

To complete

A distinctive convergence — the sound plane borrowing the graphics engine to show what it hears — though the visual vocabulary (waveform, spectrogram) is small next to a full DAW's meters and scopes.

11DSP effects palettePartial

In the engine

Correct RBJ biquad filters (lowpass / highpass / bandpass), a delay with a feedback line, gain and pan — the workhorse effects, in Zig.

Measured against

JUCE dspWeb Audio nodesFaust

To complete

No reverb (absent even as a named node), and no compressor / chorus / distortion / dynamics. Filters and delay are solid; the effect palette is thin next to JUCE or the Web Audio node set.

12Pro-audio breadth (MIDI · spatial · plugins)Emerging

In the engine

None — and stated in writing as deliberate non-goals: MIDI, music notation, 3D/HRTF spatial audio, time-stretching, and VST/CLAP plugin hosting are all "OUT until asked."

Measured against

JUCE (MIDI + VST/CLAP + spatial)Web Audio (spatial + Web MIDI)

To complete

The pro-framework breadth JUCE is chosen for — plugin hosting, MIDI, spatialization, time-stretch — is absent by choice. A real gap against JUCE, and a deliberate scope line, not an oversight.

Ce qui est dû, dans l'ordre du rendement

Les manques, classés par ce qu'ils rapportent et non par leur taille ; la valeur est l'effet sur la portée de la bibliothèque, l'effort une lecture d'ingénieur.

Tier A — fill the DSP holes

Reverb & a fuller effect palette

A reverb node (absent entirely today) plus compressor, chorus and distortion over the existing graph. The routing engine and filters are real; the effect palette is the one obvious gap in an otherwise-complete DSP.

Valeur · élevéeEffort · moyen
Richer synthesis

Wavetable, FM, additive and granular synthesis, and more envelope/LFO shapes, over the correct band-limited oscillator base — lifting synthesis from a modest set to an instrument.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen

Tier B — output breadth & the first deferred features

MP3 / OGG encode

Encode already covers WAV/FLAC and decode covers everything; adding lossy encode (MP3/OGG) closes the read-broad / write-narrow asymmetry so a processed clip can be saved in the format it arrived in.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen
MIDI input

The most-requested of the deferred set — MIDI in, to drive the synthesizer from a controller or a sequence. A natural next step once a workload asks, without reopening the whole pro-audio scope.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · moyen
GPU-FFT for large offline analysis

gpu_fft.zig exists (measured ~13× on the iGPU); wire it for offline long-transform analysis (deliberately keeping realtime DSP on CPU/SIMD), so a full-track spectrogram is fast.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · faible

Tier C — the pro-framework reach (deliberate non-goals)

Spatial / 3D / HRTF audio

Positional audio and HRTF spatialization — the immersive layer Web Audio and JUCE offer. Out of scope today; a revisit-on-demand item, not an oversight.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · élevé
Plugin hosting (VST / CLAP)

Hosting third-party audio plugins — the thing JUCE is chosen for. It would ride the (planned) extensibility plane's governed-foreign-capability model, and is deliberately deferred until asked.

Valeur · moyenneEffort · élevé
Time-stretch & pitch-shift

Independent tempo/pitch manipulation — the last of the stated non-goals, useful for the analysis and sonification lanes as much as for playback.

Valeur · faibleEffort · élevé
