Tables et données

Une table de données unifiée et large, avec un langage de motifs ; mais pas de jointure, et le groupement s'exécute hors du moteur.

2 S6 So3 P1 E

Mesuré contre: pandas · Polars · DuckDB · Dossiers: base/table · base/data · La page d'origine de l'Atlas

One enormous unified class, 2,517 methods with eleven sibling caps merged into a single stzTable, with engine-backed sort, aggregates, calculated columns, describe and pivot, plus a distinctive multi-table TableX pattern-query language. Measured against pandas, Polars and DuckDB it has three real holes: there is no join at all; group-by and filter run Haro-side even though engine bridges for both sit exported-but-unused; and the columnar engine is used per-operation, not as the live store.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • A multi-table TableX pattern-query language with no direct peer
  • A copy-free index-permutation engine sort
  • Engine-first calculated columns, compile-once over all rows, guarded at 50k rows
  • Eleven sibling caps merged into one stzTable namespace

Ce qui est dû

  • Add joins: there is no join anywhere in the tables lane
  • Wire group-by and filter to the engine bridges already compiled and exported
  • Finish the 11-to-1 fidelity grind (150 of 251 legacy tests error)
  • Missing-data toolkit (fillna / dropna) and melt / unpivot

Un exemple, exécuté

Une table se montre, se cherche et se lit par nom de colonne.

Softanza
o = new stzTable([ [ :region, :population ],
    [ "Agadez", 487620 ], [ "Maradi", 3402094 ], [ "Zinder", 3539764 ] ])
o.Show()
? @@( o.FindInCol(:region, "Maradi") )
? o.Cell(:population, 2)
