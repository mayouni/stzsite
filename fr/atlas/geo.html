L'Atlas Softanza · Visuel et spatial

Géographie et cartographie

Un moteur de cartes qui fait aussi la géodésie et les statistiques spatiales ; la plomberie SIG est la frontière.

5 S2 So4 P1 E

Mesuré contre: D3-geo · GDAL · PostGIS · Cartopy · Dossiers: base/geo · La page d'origine de l'Atlas

A genuine standout: 44 self-inverting projections, full WGS84 geodesy, and a spatstat-class spatial-statistics engine ship together behind Haro faces, guarded by ~400 assertions and 72 negative controls. It is the rare library where the tool that draws the map also computes the statistics it shows and measures its own distortion — analysis-and-cartography strong, GIS-plumbing light.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • 44 self-inverting projections with a solver that refuses a bad inverse
  • the tool that draws the map also computes the statistics and measures its own distortion
  • ordinary kriging with its variance surface
  • the Niger real-data map, vendor-free end to end

Ce qui est dû

  • vector geometry overlay algebra
  • source-CRS reprojection
  • a GeoJSON / TopoJSON writer
  • raster / DEM & GeoTIFF

Un exemple, exécuté

De Niamey à Tunis sur l'ellipsoïde, et la surface de chaque région mesurée par la routine géodésique du moteur.

Softanza
? StzGeoDistanceKm(13.51, 2.11, 36.81, 10.17)   # Niamey -> Tunis

# from niger_density, areas measured on WGS84:
#   Agadez: 0.78 /km2 (487620 people, 621917 km2)
#   Niamey: 1843.97 /km2 (1026848 people, 556 km2)
#   Agadez is 52.54% of the land, 2.85% of the people

Les couloirs, un par un

Les couloirs et leurs notes sont cités dans la langue de l'Atlas, l'anglais, tels qu'ils ont été lus à la source. lu à la source le Read from the source on 2026-09-16, from a read-only main worktree (HEAD 04cff1c34).

Map projections
Strong
44 projections across five families, all with inverses; a shared 2D Newton solver refuses any inverse not round-tripping to 1e-6; rotate/clip/fit, UTMgeo_projection.zig; all-44 inverse round-trips
Geodesy
Strong
WGS84 + ellipsoid table, geodesic inverse/direct, rhumb lines, ECEF/ENU, DMS, meridian arc, polygon area on the ellipsoid with geodesic edgesgeo_geodesy.zig
Spatial statistics
Strong
Clark-Evans z-test, Ripley K/L/G/F with Monte-Carlo envelopes, IDW, variograms, ordinary kriging with variance surface, 7+ point-process null modelsgeo_stats.zig / geo_interp.zig / geo_process.zig; kriging-variance invariance guard
Thematic maps
Strong
Choropleth (equal-area default), proportional symbol, flow (great circles), hex-bin, bloc/membership, insets with a measured locator scalestzGeoMap
Map decorations & fields
Strong
Graticule, Tissot indicatrix on the same Jacobian, a scale bar that refuses to lie, north arrow, day-night terminator, even-spaced streamlinesgeo_furniture.zig
GeoJSON / TopoJSON I/O
Solid
stzGeoFeatures reads GeoJSON and TopoJSON with an engine-side filtered read (a 40 MB file yields only wanted features); read-only, no writerstzGeoFeatures
Atlas & real-data pipeline
Solid
stzGeoAtlas binds name->shape without vendoring data; the Niger real-data map (geoBoundaries ADM1 + RGPH-2012) is the cap; no fuzzy name matchingstzGeoAtlas; test/graphics/niger_density
Geometry & spatial ops
Partial
Haro area (planar/spherical/ellipsoidal), centroid, point-in-polygon, bounding boxes, spatial join; no buffer/intersection/union/convex hull
Source reprojection (CRS transforms)
Partial
Projections are a display transform (lon/lat in, pixels out); no source-CRS reprojection from UTM or a national grid
Raster & DEM
Partial
Scalar density fields, an ESRI ASCII grid reader, per-pixel resampled field image, marching-squares contours; no hillshade/slope/aspect, no GeoTIFFgeo_field.zig
Interactivity
Partial
Maps emit interactive SVG with per-region ids, classes and <title> hooks (SetInteractive, GE2c); no live pan/zoom, by plan the GUI plane's jobSetInteractive (GE2c)
Tiling & web maps
Emerging
None: no slippy-tile scheme, no tile server, no web-map basemap integration anywhere in the geo module
