GPU

Calcul et rendu sur un même périphérique, avec une fonderie qui se règle et se vérifie elle-même ; f64 et multi-GPU refusés par choix.

5 S4 So3 P

Mesuré contre: wgpu · taichi · three.js · Dossiers: base/gpu

An in-house wgpu plane that does what neither three.js (render-only) nor taichi (compute-only) does: compute and render on one device, zero-copy. Its distinctive core is a self-tuning foundry; real consumers are accelerated and verified (t-SNE 5-8x, UMAP up to 16x, k-means 6x, neural forward 5x, exact vector search 18x), always with a bit-identical CPU fallback. What it refuses by measured decision — f64 on GPU, multi-GPU, deep 3D — are scope boundaries, not oversights.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • a self-tuning, self-checking calibration foundry: category-of-one
  • compute and render zero-copy on one device
  • every GPU route measured, verified, and reversible to CPU
  • byte-identical WGSL native vs browser

Ce qui est dû

  • broaden the kernel transpiler beyond elementwise
  • whole-pipeline GPU residency
  • idle-aware routing (GK1b)
  • deeper 3D rendering
