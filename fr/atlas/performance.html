Performance

Ferme la triade OpenTelemetry, le contrat de service et l'analyse de capacité en un seul plan ; export seulement, ni stockage ni tableaux de bord.

6 S5 So1 P

Mesuré contre: OpenTelemetry · Prometheus · Dossiers: base/perf · La page d'origine de l'Atlas

One of the most complete and rigorous planes in the library -- P0-P11 all shipped, 366 assertions -- it closes the OTel triad (metrics + traces + logs, all in native OTLP JSON) and exposes Prometheus text, and adds SLA judgment with edge-triggered alerting, a sampling profiler, queueing-theory capacity analysis and a driven-load harness, in one coherent plane. Its real signature is measurement discipline: clocks as scopes, Windows 15.6 ms quantization handled, self-cost priced, and a rule that unmeasured is an error, never a silent pass. What it is not, by design, is the storage-and-dashboard half.

Ce qu'un maker en fait, et ce qui se distingue

  • Closes the OTel triad (metrics + traces + logs) and exposes Prometheus text, in one plane
  • Measurement discipline: clocks as scopes, Windows 15.6 ms quantization handled, self-cost priced with its own machinery
  • Unmeasured is an error, never a silent pass
  • Queueing-theory capacity analysis with independent self-checks (utilization, Little's law)

Ce qui est dû

  • Auto-instrumentation (zero-code, opt-out rather than opt-in Observe)
  • Full trace-context propagation (baggage, async-context through reactor jobs, per-task scopes)
  • Head/tail trace sampling; in-process aggregation (PromQL-lite); native-stack profiling
  • A storage/dashboard integration story; macOS RSS; one stale CLAUDE.md metadata line (P0-P7 / 283 vs P0-P11 / 366)

Les couloirs, un par un

Les couloirs et leurs notes sont cités dans la langue de l'Atlas, l'anglais, tels qu'ils ont été lus à la source. lu à la source le Read from the source on 2026-09-16, from a read-only main worktree.

Metrics & labeled families
Strong
Counters/gauges/timers and Prometheus-style labeled families with the child registry engine-side; cardinality bounded at birth with a counted overflow child.perf.zig Family; stzMetric, stzMetricFamily
Percentiles & histograms
Strong
Two independent engines: an O(1) log-bucket sketch and sort-exact nearest-rank over the window; histogram_sum keeps an exact lifetime sum so the mean is exact.histogram.zig, perf_series_percentile
Exposition & export
Strong
Prometheus text (PromText) and OTLP JSON for metrics, spans and logs; built-in GET /metrics + /health; pull (scrape) and push (OTLP) both present.stzOtelBatch, stzLog.OtelJson
SLA judgment & alerting
Strong
stzSla verdicts in the unified rule shape into stzRuleReport; stzPerfSentinel edge-triggered OnBreach/OnClear with a flight recorder; NOT-MEASURED is an error.stzSla, stzPerfSentinel, stzRuleReport
Measurement discipline
Strong
Clocks as scopes, Windows 15.6 ms quantization handled, SelfCost() priced (~0.03 ms), exact-transfer proven against frozen stubs, the self-check identity law.watch.zig; perf laws in CLAUDE.md
Capacity & queueing analysis
Strong
stzPerfProfile: utilization, throughput, service demand, MaxThroughput, Headroom, Bottleneck split, memory trend, Little's law with an independent self-check.stzPerfProfile
Distributed tracing
Solid
W3C traceparent spans, parent->child join, engine trace circular buffer, OTLP resourceSpans export; propagation at the HTTP seam only, one global scope, drop-oldest circular buffer.stzOtelBatch
Log<->trace correlation
Solid
Inside stzTraceScope every stzLog record stamps a traceId; OfTrace(id) recovers a trip's lines; OTLP logs export; one global scope slot.stzTraceScope, stzLog
The instrumentation bracket
Solid
Observe/ObserveRoutes wrap the appserver seam recording the well-known subjects; stzStopwatch brackets any operation; manual, no auto-instrumentation.Observe, ObserveRoutes, stzStopwatch
Profiling
Solid
stzProfiler over an engine frame stack plus a real engine sampler thread, folded flamegraph output; cooperative Enter/Leave frames, not native stack unwinding.stzProfiler
Governed tuning & load harness
Solid
stzPerfPlan: a closed catalog an LLM may propose, only an effectful actor may ExecuteOn; stzLoadDriver measured X sub-linear and R super-linear on a real fleet.stzPerfPlan, stzLoadDriver
Storage, query & dashboards
Partial
Bounded in-memory rings hold the recent window; no time-series database, no PromQL, no dashboards -- export-only by explicit design.
