What the foundation does that the areas do not say

The foundation

The Atlas lists twenty-eight areas. Five things cut across all of them: one graph beneath every domain, one function that runs at four widths, everything showing itself, everything proven, and a long reach. One screen each.

  • The graph beneath

    Code, workflows, knowledge, schemas and a declared world are one structure.

  • Four widths

    One function, run on lanes, cores, the graphics card or nodes, by measurement.

  • Visual by default

    Whatever is computed can show itself, with nothing added.

  • Proven

    Tests, scenario guards, fuzzers, property suites, a gate and a bill of materials.

  • The declared languages

    Every file you declare, in one table, with its stage.

  • Reach

    Platforms, other languages, and the ports to the world.
