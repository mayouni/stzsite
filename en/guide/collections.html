Finding

  • Find Find the given item: the positions of EVERY occurrence, as a list (engine-backed). (case-sensitive by default) in stzList
  • FindWF FindAllItemsW: the "extended" W scan. Unlike the plain W form, it -- accepts the expressive Softanza keywords (@NextItem, @PreviousItem, -- @NextNumber, ...) and the Q(EXPR).Method(...) predicate form. The -- condition is transpiled to the basic This[@i+1]/This[@i-1] indexing -- and any Q(...) predicate is lowered to engine DSL; the scan is then -- bounded to the "executable section" so navigation indices stay in -- range (e.g. with @NextItem the last position is excluded, since it -- has no successor). Returns the matching 1-based POSITIONS. -- WF: anonymous-function constraints (full Haro power, no eval) in stzList
  • FindNth Case-insensitive word-order aliases used by narrative tests. (Cannot live inside FindNthOccurrenceCS as nested defs because they take a different arity -- top-level methods instead.) in stzList
  • NthItem getting the nth item in the list in stzList
  • FindItem The positions of every occurrence of the item (the Find contract). in stzList
  • FindLast essential methods for submodule support in stzList
  • FindNext FindNext: convenience wrapper used by narrative tests. Accepts either FindNext(item, n) or FindNext(item, :StartingAt = n). in stzList
  • LastItem getting the last item in the list in stzList
  • FindItems [[item, [positions...]], ...] in first-appearance order in stzList
  • FindPairs singles (1-elem lists) and pairs (2-elem lists) in stzList
  • FirstHalf The first floor(n/2) items of the list. in stzList
  • FirstItem getting the first item in the list in stzList
