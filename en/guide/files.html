Files & formats

You reach a file by what you intend to do with it, and each format is parsed once, in the engine, by a parser whose refusals are part of its safety.

The functions, by what they do

Testing and checking

  • AreFiles batch validation methods in stzFolder
  • AreFolders batch validation methods in stzFolder
  • IsSecurePath checking path security in stzFolder
  • IsValid A document is valid if it parsed and yielded at least one element. in stzHtml

Finding

  • Find Find: minimal CSS-like dispatch (tag / #id / .class). Returns a list of stzHtmlNode handles bound to this document. in stzHtml

Counting and measuring

  • CountFileMatches matchings in stzFolder
  • CountFolderMatches matchings in stzFolder
  • NumberOfElements Count + accessors over the flat element index. in stzHtml

Removing

  • Delete DELETE OPERATIONS in stzFileManager

Adding

  • AddFile MODIFICATION OPERATIONS in stzZipFile
  • AddElement a leaf element with text. in stzXmlBuilder

Splitting and extracting

  • SplitByLines SPLIT OPERATIONS in stzFileManager
  • ExtractTo EXTRACTION OPERATIONS in stzZipFile

Ordering and moving

  • MoveTo MOVE OPERATIONS in stzFileManager

Converting

  • ToHtml Serialise this node (tag, attrs, text then children) to HTML. in stzHtmlBuildNode

Reading and writing

  • Write WRITE METHODS (intent-specific) in stzFileAppender
  • WriteSeperator @Misspelled in stzFileAppender
  • Open open a container; every Add... until Close goes inside it. in stzXmlBuilder
