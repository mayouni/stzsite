Why Softanza

Declare a language

In the agentic era you do not write software. You declare worlds in languages made for their domains, and you govern how those worlds change.

The act

The kernel of the whole Softanza vision is one generative act:

Declare a language.

Not "use our language": declare yours. The language of your domain, in your jargon, and the machinery returns it as a true language: executable, judged, governed, intelligent.

The workshop practised this act five times before naming it once: a query language (ZQL), an interface language (Zui, twenty-four closed verbs and a constitution of one hundred and seventeen rules), an expression language (W), a meta-grammar where "every valid Zin declaration is a ZML declaration", and a workflow language. Declaring a language became as easy as declaring a variable.

The era thesis

In the agentic era you do not write software: you declare worlds in languages made for their domains, and you govern how those worlds change. Humans and AIs meet in declared languages, small, closed, judged, and not in general-purpose code.

The feature no language workbench ever had, and the reason this factory wins where they died: every declared grammar also emits its constraint grammar, and the engine's constrained decoder makes a violating token unemittable. An agent is physically constrained to speak only valid sentences of the language. Malformedness dies by construction. Falsehood still faces the court: structure kills malformedness, never falsehood.

The family sentence

You write your language, the forge forges it, the runtime runs it, the projector projects it, the external door reaches out, all on one engine, all judged, and the narrator narrates it back.

Seven organs, one letter each: the engine, the runtime, the forge, the projector, the door to foreign languages, your language, and the narrator. The narrator is the voice of every court: every run narrates itself, and no output is ever stored.

The inverse asset

The honest comparison is Wolfram, and it is not symmetric. Wolfram's moat is symbolic computation and decades of curated world knowledge. We will not win that frontally, not with more effort, not later, and this site will never claim to.

Wolfram knows the world's facts. Softanza knows your world.

Softanza's world is the declared entities, fields, actors, norms, flows, screens and processes of your organisation, plus the committed ledger of what actually happened under them. A Softanza notebook can ask questions no notebook in the world can ask, for a structural reason: its dependency graph and your project's symbol table are the same graph. Which flows touch the deposit entity, and what breaks if I add a field? Which actors can see this, under which norms? Wolfram can tell you the melting point of tungsten. It cannot tell you any of that about your company, because it was never given your company.

Where we do not compete

Symbolic mathematics, pure research computing, and anything whose value is the curated corpus. Those are Wolfram's, they are defensible, and pretending otherwise would waste the only advantage we have, which is knowing what our advantage is.
