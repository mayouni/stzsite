The engine

The engine is written in Zig: 375 source files, 160,063 lines. The largest, with their lines: list (6,518), string (5,052), ring_bridge_stats (4,476), ring_bridge_string (4,335), eigen_general (4,254), linalg (3,309), reactor (2,969), graph (2,808).

The tests

5,284 test files in 158 folders, of which 527 are scenario guards and 4,474 are numbered tests of the classic form.
