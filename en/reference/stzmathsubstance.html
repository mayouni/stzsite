SetData
data ------------------------------------------------------------------ A NUMBER ON AN OBJECT. Penrose's Substance carries no numbers, and for a set or a point that is right: the content is the relation, not the coordinate. A table is different -- a cell IS its row, its column and its value -- and a heatmap is nothing but numbers. SetData puts one on an object under a key; any Style expression reads it as "x.key", to drive a position or, through a colour rule, a fill.QCSXTZZZIBWU
