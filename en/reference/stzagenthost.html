SetActionBudget
One agent acting in a loop floods everything downstream of it -- the cascading failure. A budget caps the ACTIONS (skill firings: what causes effects) an agent may take in a sliding window. An agent that exceeds it is QUARANTINED, not merely slowed: a runaway is a containment event, it stays stopped until an effectful actor releases it, and the reason says how far over it went. Both pumps account.
