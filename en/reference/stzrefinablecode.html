As
EXECUTION TRUST POSTURES (5.8) -------------------------------------- A posture says WHERE a refinement came from. Trust rank (high -> low): :trusted (3, in-process/verified) > :external (2, an external tool) > :sandboxed (1, isolated run) > :llm (0, LLM-composed). A proposal defaults to :trusted; As(posture) declares otherwise for the next To(). The posture rides into the audit chain; a point's TrustFloor refuses anything below the required rank (LLM-composed edits to a critical knob can be forbidden without forbidding a human's).
