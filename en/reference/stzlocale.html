stzLocale

120 own methods · 1 forms written with extensions, listed under their method

the source: base/i18n/stzLocale.ring · All classes · The guide Internationalization

An extension is written at the end of a method's name: it is the same method with one more word, never another method. Each method says below which exist: a bold, ticked name exists for it, a struck name does not. Q chains · CS case sensitivity · XT extended · Z positions · ZZ sections · IB bounds · W condition · U unique · The extensions and what they mean
