routing: RETRY-WITH-FAILOVER + per-worker CIRCUIT BREAKER ----------- Proxy to a healthy worker of pcTag. Path is SSRF/CRLF-validated first.no extension

ScaleDown
GRACEFUL drain-down: mark one ready worker of a profile DRAINING -- routing stops immediately (it finishes in-flight work), and the process self-exits on its TTL. Returns the drained port, or 0 if there is nothing to drain. (Forced kill of a hung worker is a small future engine add; drain is the graceful path.)no extension

ForceKill
forced kill + orphan cleanup (the forceful sibling of drain) -------- Force-kill EVERY spawned worker of a facet (SIGKILL). The forceful counterpart to ScaleDown's graceful drain: use it on a WEDGED worker that neither answers health nor self-exits on its TTL. Marks each not ready and returns how many were actually killed. External workers (RegisterExternalWorker, jobId 0) are NOT ours to kill and are skipped.no extension
