SetWorld
== 1. GENERATION ======================================================== SET the world this platform envelopes -- by HARVESTING its declarations into the platform's own records, right now. It does NOT hold the world: Haro copies an object into an attribute, so a held stzApp would be a dead snapshot the moment the caller touched it (see the aliasing note in the header). Call this AFTER the world is declared. The world is ASKED through its own accessors -- a platform never reaches into another object's attributes. (A constellation ADDs many worlds; a platform SETs its one.)no extension
