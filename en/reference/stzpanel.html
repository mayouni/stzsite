FromTexture
A panel hanging in a 3D scene is hit by a RAY, and what the caller has after the intersection is a texture coordinate. This is the whole of the in-scene input mapping (§6's tier): uv in, panel pixels out. v is flipped because a texture's origin is bottom-left and a panel's is top-left -- the one place that difference is stated, so no caller has to remember it.
