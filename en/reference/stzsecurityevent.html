OccurredAt
The deterministic form (the house "...At(now)" convention): an event that did not happen NOW carries its own wall clock -- a replayed or imported record, or a guard that needs exact window arithmetic instead of machine speed. The monotonic stamp is left alone: it orders THIS process's events and cannot be borrowed.

Failed

Observed
NOT A VERDICT -- something simply happened (incident I2's session seams). A session reaching its expiry was neither granted nor refused: no gate ran, nobody was told no. Forcing such a fact into "refused" would have made every pivot that counts refusals over-count, and would have taught an investigator that routine housekeeping was an attack. The ledger's job is to witness, and some of what it witnesses carries no judgment at all.
