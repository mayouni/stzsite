REVERSIBILITY CLASS (the sixth contract, ruling 3.2) ---------------- How UNDOABLE a subject's work is -- orthogonal to how RISKY it is (contract 1): a tier-1 action that cannot be undone deserves more ceremony than a tier-3 action that can.
