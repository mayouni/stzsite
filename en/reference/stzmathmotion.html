the export: a storyboard driven by the same acts ------------------------ EVERY STATE A FRAME. The storyboard takes its own copy of the picture from where it stands now, replays each state's acts on that copy through its own Act (so the frame it closes is the picture AFTER the acts), binds each fact to be computed on that frame, writes the frame as a PNG in the folio, and the narration beside them.
