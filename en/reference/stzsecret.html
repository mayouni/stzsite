RevealVia
resolver-aware reveal: a vault-sourced secret is fetched THROUGH a resolver, passed by reference at reveal time (after the actor gate). ANY object with a Resolve(locator) method is a valid resolver -- your vault client, or the reference stzVaultResolver. For a non-vault secret the resolver is ignored and this behaves like Reveal(actor).
