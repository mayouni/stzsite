CaptureInsteadOfDevice
WHAT IT CANNOT STAND IN FOR: a device drains on ITS OWN thread, so while this thread is busy rendering a new loop's notes the card keeps playing what was posted. Here THIS thread is the consumer, so it stalls with the render and nothing is consumed. Whether a render while playing costs a note its frame is therefore a DEVICE question, and the guard asks it on the device.
