Reduce()

Reduce(): 0-arg auto-concat / auto-sum. - All-string items: concatenate. - All-number items: sum. - Mixed: concatenate stringified items.

Basic examples (1)

Softanza
# Reduce() of [1,2,3,4] is 10 (numeric sum)
? Q([1,2,3,4]).Reduce()
# ReduceXT('@accumulator + @item', 0) on [1..4] is 10
? Q([1,2,3,4]).ReduceXT("@accumulator + @item", 0)
# ReduceXT with init 100 is 110
? Q([1,2,3,4]).ReduceXT("@accumulator + @item", 100)
# ReduceNoInit('@accumulator * @item') on [1..4] is 24
? Q([1,2,3,4]).ReduceNoInit("@accumulator * @item")
Output
10
10
110
24
