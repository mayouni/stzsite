A NOTE IS MOVED BY ITS OFFSETS. Its place is derived from its mark plus two unknowns, n.ox and n.oy (the free side is the mark's datum), which is what lets every note start inside its leash; a drag by shape (DragTo) wants a free centre and refuses it.
