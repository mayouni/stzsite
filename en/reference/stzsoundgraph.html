stzSoundGraph

31 own methods · 16 forms written with extensions, listed under their method

the source: base/sound/stzSoundGraph.ring(opens in a new tab) · All classes · The area reference: Sound

A sound described as a graph: sources, shapers, mixes and an output, shaped like the Web Audio graph and rendered by the engine, bus by planar bus.

o1 = new stzSoundGraph()

# a sine at 440 hertz, then a master gain on top
o1.AddOscillator(:Sine, 440, 0.5)
o1.AddMasterGain()

? o1.NodeCount()
#--> 2
run in the library on 2026-10-21

Sources are named by their waveform, sine, triangle, square or saw, or are a sound already held, a looping sound, or a timeline of notes each placed at its frame. Shapers take what they act on by name: gain, filters, envelopes that may start some seconds in, echo. Echo is called echo and not reverb: one repeat fading out is not a room, and a name that promises a hall and delivers one repeat is a false name. Nodes are named, an unknown name is an error that the caller hears about, and the output is the node chosen, by default the last.

A rendered graph has no device in its loop: the buffer it produces can be measured, saved or played. It serves synthesis, sound design, and every tone, cue and score of the library, which compile down to this graph.
