Submit
the one writer of progress: a check that RAN THE FACT SHAPE. The knowledge graph keeps ONE edge per pair of nodes, so `ali | tried | ex` and `ali | passed | ex` cannot both exist -- found by this plane's guard, 2026-09-23. Every fact about an attempt therefore hangs off its own subject, and each carries a distinct object (the prefixes keep two equal hashes from being one node): ali | worked-on | ex-01-01 ex-01-01-by-ali | verdict | passed (passed | diverged | error) ex-01-01-by-ali | last-attempt | attempt:<sha256> ex-01-01-by-ali | evidence | sha256:<sha256> (only when passed) ex-01-01-by-ali | checked-at-ms | <epoch ms, UTC> (only when passed)no extension
