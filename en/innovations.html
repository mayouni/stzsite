The register

The innovations

Forty-seven ideas of Softanza that are its own, each with the record's one line, its evidence and the pages that tell it. Three of them open the list, and some are not told on this site yet.

The Atlas is an anatomy of areas, and a paradigm has no area: it cuts across them. This register is where those ideas are listed together. The three that open it, scope-oriented programming, Reaxis and the domain language model, were named by the author as the ones to put under the light.

designed An outside assessment read four inventories of the library at commit 93d7a39 on 2026-10-07 (the narrations, the design documents, the engine's twelve paradigm engines, an outside reader's list) and crossed them with the code and the guards. The evidence column counts files that name a thing, not passing tests. Row 47 was added later, when answering by example was written up.
