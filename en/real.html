The proving grounds

Real applications

An application is real when a document proves it and a person uses it. Three grounds have shaped Softanza, each with what it holds and what it asked of the library.

Three grounds

Organizium, Niamey (built, in use)

A bank's organisation modelled before its application: its platforms, its security, its norms. The Standard Edition runs under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, and the regulators' rules, the BCEAO's and Basel III's, are validators of its chart. See customers.

RestoLean, Lyon (built, first release)

A platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant and demonstrated on a real phone. Its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. A constellation with a commons and several worlds, one for the customer's phone. See forged in projects.

DIKO Hub (designed)

A design study in Niger that links an organisation's tools. It is not a delivery. What it asked of the library is the payments port, which exists because of it. See forged in projects.

in construction Nothing else is a reference until a document proves it. A project is listed with its stage and not with the number of users or countries it is said to reach.
