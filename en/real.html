What Softanza was made to carry

Projects and references

Three projects shaped Softanza. Two are references, each attested by a document; the third is a design study. Each is told here once, for a customer: what was asked, what exists, what it proved and its stage. What the library learned from each is on Forged in projects.

Three projects

Organizium, Niamey (built, in use)

Asked: to model a bank's organisation before its application, and to judge it by its own chart: its platforms, its security, its norms. What exists: the audit, the referential, a training module, and the Standard Edition under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, with the regulators' rules, the BCEAO's and Basel III's, as validators of its chart. Proved: an organisation can be judged by its own chart.

Reference: the installation guide delivered to the bank · what the library learned

RestoLean, Lyon (built, first release)

Asked: a platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant. What exists: a mission of analysis in 2024, specification stories written as narrations, then an application that runs, shown on a real phone by the owner himself: a constellation with a commons and several worlds, one for the customer's phone. Its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. Proved: a specification is not a delivery.

Reference: amendment no. 1, closed on 4 August 2026 · what the library learned

DIKO, Niamey (design study)

Asked: a platform that speaks an organisation's own language and links its tools. What exists: a design study, not a delivery. Proved: it caused the payments port the library now has.
