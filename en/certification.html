Certification

A certification here would be evidence and not an examination: a project whose guards pass, and a human review of it. This page says what is proposed, why it is built that way, and that nothing is certified today.

What is proposed

For a person (designed)

A level of the ladder, earned by a project folder whose guards pass, and read by a human. The pass carries the fingerprint of the work, so anyone can check that the work is the work.

For a company (designed)

A certified partner would be one that has delivered a project under review, with its stages said as they are.

Why it is built that way

The learning system refuses certificates that are only the result of an examination. A certificate that anyone can pass by memorising proves memory. A project whose guards pass, and that a person has read, proves that the work was done and that someone vouched for it.

What is not decided

designed This is a proposal of 30 September 2026. Nothing is certified, and no person or company holds a Softanza certification. The proposal needs an axis of its own in the project's reference design before it is announced as an offering, and the site does not announce it. No price, no fee and no share is stated, because none is set. See the platform architect's journey for the work such a certification would examine.
