A direction, with its stages

Conversations

Your users keep their medium. A world declared once could reach them in the messaging app on their phone, in a text menu on any phone, or by voice, with the same court judging every sentence. This page says what exists for that, what is designed, and what is absent.

Why

Messaging is a gap twice: as a computational unit, the message, and as a medium, the app a person already uses. The people Softanza serves already have a medium. A restaurant owner's tools were his memory, a paper notebook, a spreadsheet and some apps, among them a messaging app, and his requirement was an interface in French, simple as a messaging app, never more than two gestures. The first demonstration of that project ran on a real phone, by the owner himself, over a messaging app, in August 2026.

The aim is conversations that happen programmatically by default, so that a designer has them as a first-class interface and does not oblige users to change their habits.

What exists

The governed conversation (built)

A multi-turn exchange with state, inside a knowledge space. The gap between the shape the knowledge needs and what it holds generates the next question. The floor is deterministic and needs no model. It runs on the wise coding page.

The transcript and the goal (built)

Every line has a speaker, a text and a certainty. A goal is the gap that generates the questions.

Ports with sandboxes (built)

A text-message port is any object that can send a number and a text. Its sandbox records what would have been sent and delivers nothing, so the outbox becomes something a test can assert on. The mail port is the same shape.

A registry that refuses a fake (built)

A service is declared, a sandbox is bound to it while developing, the live service at deployment, and production refuses the fake.

Natural language and voice (built)

The natural layer reads English, French, Arabic, Hausa and Turkish. Speech synthesis exists in the engine.

The bill, run

A text message is billed per segment, and the segment count is not the character count: it depends on the alphabet. The sandbox counts the bill and names the encoding, so a defect that hides in a template shows up before a campaign is paid for.

The honesty figure: a text message is billed per segment, and one emoji added to a message of 100 characters doubles its cost, as does Arabic past 70 letters. The sandbox counts the bill before any subscription exists.

cAdded = StzRepeatStr("a", 100) + StzUnicodeToChar(128512)
# 100 plain characters
? StzSmsSegments(StzRepeatStr("a", 100))[:segments]
# the same message with one emoji added
? StzSmsSegments(cAdded)[:segments]

The bridge

The interface law of this site states that its grammar is closed: a new medium adds renderings, never verbs. A conversation renders the same verbs the interface has. To discover is a menu. To select is "reply 2". To confirm is "reply YES". To undo is "reply CANCEL". To scroll is "reply MORE". A messaging thread, a text-menu screen and a voice prompt are media, and a world declared once would project to them as it projects to the web and the phone.

The commons service (designed)

The constellation's design lists messaging among the commons: identity, ledger, payments, messaging. It is one line in a design, and no code carries the word yet.

A facet per medium (designed)

Each medium is a target of the world, with a matrix as its court: a verb a medium cannot render is a red cell that demands a fallback, never a silent omission.

The court of what is said (designed)

Approved templates as a closed grammar, an agent allowed to emit only admitted sentences, and the transcript kept as evidence in the ledger.

What is absent

  • The message as a unit: its parts, its reply, its medium, its status.
  • The channel ports and their sandboxes for a messaging app, a text menu on any phone, voice and web chat. A text menu is a session of short screens with a timeout, which a text message is not.
  • A session with its window and its timeout as states, consent as data, and approved templates.
  • Speech to text and telephony.
  • Zarma and Fulfulde in the natural layer.

in construction No conversational medium is a target today: the application layer targets the mobile, the desktop and the web. This page is a direction and says so.
