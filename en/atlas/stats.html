Statistics & data-viz

A careful compute core and byte-tested charts; nonparametrics, resampling and half the chart types owed.

2 Strong · 4 Solid · 3 Partial · 3 Emerging

Measured against: R · scipy.stats · statsmodels · matplotlib · Folders: base/stats

The compute core is genuinely careful -- SIMD Neumaier summation, engine hypothesis tests that never return a bare p-value, and a chart pipeline byte-tested against captured ground truth. But this is not yet scipy.stats or R: nonparametric tests, resampling and time-series are absent, half the analyst's chart types don't render, and the exploratory (Tukey) tier is a plan with no code. A strong foundation, honestly incomplete.

What a maker does with it, and what stands out

  • Lane-parallel Neumaier compensated summation (measured 2.5x)
  • Hypothesis tests never return a bare p-value: effect size and a conclusion with every one
  • Chart renderers byte-tested against captured ground truth (107 parity assertions)
  • Three backends per chart: engine terminal, deviceless SVG, GPU PNG

What is owed

  • Nonparametric and normality tests (Mann-Whitney, Kruskal-Wallis, KS, Shapiro-Wilk)
  • The statistical chart family (box, violin, pie/donut, heatmap, area)
  • Bootstrap CI and permutation test over the existing sampler
  • Multiple regression, GLM and diagnostics; a real time-series lane; the unbuilt Tukey tier

The lanes, one by one

Lanes and their notes are quoted as they were read at the source, on Read from the source on 2026-09-16.

Descriptive statistics
Strong
Full descriptive set over lane-parallel Neumaier compensated summation (@Vector(8), measured 2.5x); at parity and numerically more careful than most.stats.zig
Chart rendering pipeline & fidelity
Strong
Three backends per chart; renderers guarded byte-identical against captured ground truth (107 parity assertions), 19 SVG/PNG checks with no GPU.plot.zig; 107 parity assertions + 19 deviceless SVG/PNG checks
Hypothesis testing
Solid
t-tests, chi-square, one-way ANOVA, Pearson test each return statistic, df, p-value, effect size and a conclusion; the nonparametric family is missing.hypothesis.zig
Multivariate & dimensionality reduction
Solid
PCA, t-SNE/UMAP, k-means, kNN, naive Bayes, ID3, logistic regression, FFT, eigen-solver over a 15 GFLOP/s matmul; shallower than scikit-learn.pca.zig, cluster.zig, fft.zig
Analytical dataset & wrangling
Solid
stzDataSet (211 methods): CIs, five-number stats, contingency, entropy, mutual information, insights/rules/plans; stzDataWrangler is pure Haro.stzDataSet, stzDataWrangler
Optimization & solvers
Solid
Real LP/MIP: Big-M tableau simplex in the engine, branch-and-bound, stochastic/robust solver, root/quadrature, LU/Gauss-Jordan.simplex.zig, optim.zig, stzLinearSolver
Distribution functions
Partial
CDF and quantile for Normal, Student-t, Chi-square, F only; no discrete distributions (binomial, Poisson), no distribution object.special.zig
Correlation & regression
Partial
Pearson/Spearman, covariance, simple linear regression engine-backed; no multiple regression, GLM, logistic-as-stats or ridge/lasso.frame.zig
Charts & data-viz catalogue
Partial
Bar, histogram, scatter, treemap render across all three backends; box, violin, pie/donut, heatmap, area, radar, contour all missing.stzPlotCanvas
Resampling & Monte-Carlo inference
Emerging
random.zig ships seeded sampling and shuffles but no bootstrap CI, permutation test or jackknife is built on it.random.zig
Time-series
Emerging
Moving average is an engine primitive, trend is Haro-side; no autocorrelation, seasonality, exponential smoothing or ARIMA.stz_stats_moving_average
Exploratory data analysis (Tukey tier)
Emerging
A detailed plan of record (TK0-TK6) with no code shipped: no eda.zig, no faces, no test/tukey/.SOFTANZA_TUKEY_PLAN.md
