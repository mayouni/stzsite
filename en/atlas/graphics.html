The Softanza Atlas · Visual & spatial

Graphics

One Zig engine across twelve lanes, text, colour, cartography, GPU, under one governed system.

6 S3 So2 P1 E

Measured against: Skia · D3 · Graphviz · Folders: base/graphics · The original Atlas page

Most of the field ships one lane well: Skia renders, D3 charts the web, Graphviz lays out graphs, spatstat does the statistics and draws nothing. Softanza is one Zig engine behind declarative Haro faces that reaches across twelve of those lanes at once; its advantage is being coherent across twelve lanes under one governed colour and text system, which no single competitor is.

What a maker does with it, and what stands out

  • coherent across twelve lanes under one governed colour and text system
  • Arabic kashida justification at font-declared points
  • the rare lane where a graphics library also computes the statistics it draws
  • compute and render zero-copy on one wgpu device

What is owed

  • general paths, curves & transforms
  • complete the chart family
  • colour-blindness simulation
  • PDF export & the raster floor

The lanes, one by one

Lanes and their notes are quoted as they were read at the source. read from the source on Read from the source on 2026-09-15.

Text & typography
Strong
SheenBidi -> HarfBuzz -> stb_truetype: bidi reorder, OpenType shaping, Arabic kashida justification at font-declared points, fallback chains, vertical CJK
Colour system
Strong
OKLCH/Oklab throughout, monotonic ramps, a five-value semantic family under a constitution, ten themes, WCAG 2 + APCA contrast as gate-able numbersStzIsLegible / StzContrastLc; SOFTANZA_COLOR_SYSTEM.md
Cartography & projections
Strong
44 projections with inverses, WGS84 geodesy, GeoJSON/TopoJSON, choropleth/symbol/flow/hex-bin, insets, Tissot, scale bar, north arrow, terminatorstz_geo.dll
Spatial statistics
Strong
Clark-Evans z-test, Ripley K/L, G & F, envelopes, kernel density, contours, IDW, variograms, ordinary kriging with variance; seven null models
Diagramming
Strong
25+ notations all closed (DN0-DN24), many constraint-solved; own deterministic layout engine (GG1-GG6) plus a live picture-as-input modeSOFTANZA_GRAPH_PLANE_PLAN.md (DN0-DN24, GG1-GG6)
GPU compute + render
Strong
In-house wgpu surface: instanced multi-texture render and compute (matmul, fft, softmax, topk, pairdist) zero-copy on one device, W-string -> WGSLstz_gpu.dll
2D vector rendering
Solid
Retained display-list canvas with an SVG floor that always answers, GPU-backed PNG; no Bezier/arc/AddPath, no transform stack, CPU rasteriser never written
Accessibility
Solid
An accessibility tree with roles, names, reading order and ToJSON, a screen-reader bridge, contrast maths gating colour; CVD simulation owed
Output & portability
Solid
SVG everywhere, lossless PNG with a returned encode decision, native window and browser tiers; no PDF/JPEG/WebP, only Windows runtime-verified
Charts & data-viz
Partial
Bar, multi-bar, line, scatter, histogram, surface, treemap with SVG/PNG/terminal renderers; no pie/donut, area, heatmap, box/violin, candlestickstzDataSet
Interactivity & animation
Partial
Canvas hit-testing (Pick), SVG ids/classes for hover, a drag-driven live diagram; animation and tweening explicitly deferred to GUI G5
Perception — the plane can draw, not yet see
Emerging
VISION plane's first rung: a draw->read round trip through Windows.Media.Ocr and onnxruntime (10 ms, glyph-accurate incl. Arabic shaping)
