The declared languages

In Softanza you declare, and what you declare is a file in a small language made for the subject. Here they are in one table: what each declares, and how far each has come.

LanguageWhat it declaresStageWhere
Wa condition inside an argumentbuiltThe craft
.zknwwhat a world or an agent knows: entities, relations, rulesbuiltWise coding
.piaan agent: what it covers, how reversible its acts are, its skillsbuiltThe agent
.stzapp, .stzplatforman application, and a platform made of thembuilt (the format)Applications
.machine, .fleet, .packa machine, the links between machines, the servers placed on onebuilt, emulatedThe platform architect
.zgova governance regime: who may commit whatbuiltSecurity
.zcnva conversation, as a recordbuiltConversations
.panelan interface, authored as text and drawn by a native rendererin constructionSurfaces
.wflowa workflowin constructionThe analyst and the designer

.stzna notebook of cellsnamedThe estate

The Softanza way A language is named by its file, and a file is judged by a court when it is loaded. What you write is plain text you own, and a declaration that the court refuses never runs.

in construction Each row is a reading of the library and of the charters and READMEs of the estate's other repositories, by an outside assessment on 2026-10-07, and not a run. The grammar and the court of each language are told on the page in the last column; where there is no such page yet, the row is the whole account.
