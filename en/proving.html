What Softanza was made to carry

Proving grounds

Three projects have been the proving grounds of Softanza. Each is told here for a customer first: what was asked, what exists, what was learned. The lessons for the library are on the Forged page.

Three grounds

RestoLean, Lyon (built, first release)

Asked: a platform for neighbourhood commerce, carried by the owner of a couscous restaurant. What exists: a mission of analysis in 2024, specification stories written as narrations, then an application that runs, shown on a real phone by the owner himself; its ergonomic rule is at most two gestures per action. Learned: a specification is not a delivery.

What the library learned

Organizium, Niamey (built, in use)

Asked: to model a bank's organisation before its application, and to judge it by its own chart. What exists: the audit, the referential, a training module, and the Standard Edition under licence at the Sonibank, on the bank's internal network, with the regulators' rules as validators of the chart. Learned: an organisation can be judged by its own chart.

What the library learned · the reference's document

DIKO, Niamey (design study)

Asked: a platform that speaks an organisation's own language. What exists: a design study, not a delivery. Learned: it caused the payments port the library now has.

What the library learned
