Products

The family

Every product carries its stage, and the stage is a word you can check: built, charter, specification, ratified proposal, named. A product that does not exist yet says so.

Stage legend: built the code exists and its guards pass · charter the design document exists, nothing is built · specification the document and, sometimes, a prototype · ratified proposal the direction is decided, the charter waits · named a name, and nothing else yet. Stages read on 2026-09-30 in the repositories themselves.

Softanza built

The foundation: the library and its Zig engine, over three hundred thousand lines, 5,822 commits on the main branch, public on GitHub and Codeberg. Everything else on this page stands on it.

github.com/mayouni/stzlib · codeberg.org/MAyouni/stzlib

The Learning System built

A module of the foundation: two courses of fifteen chapters in four languages, missions, projects, institution overlays, cohorts, a tutor; eleven guards. Shipped between 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-24.

base/education

Haro in construction

The platform's language: designed for an agent to write and a human to govern, on a sovereign register virtual machine and compiler in Zig that already run the platform's code. Its charter v0.1 of 2026-09-26 awaits the author's ratification. This site will never say Haro is available before it is.

the virtual machine's repository

Harobanda built

The declared machine: you describe the whole computer in one text file, it boots exactly that, and checks every boot against the file. Built for AI agents you must be able to govern. Boots in an emulator today; public under the MIT licence since 2026-09-27.

github.com/mayouni/harobanda

HaroBase specification

The family's governed data store: plain SQLite, in the process, governed; a Zig library with a C interface shared by every face. Charter ratified 2026-09-29; the first two layers are built and their guards pass. Private repository for now.

Aïcha named

The name the author gives the Softanza language model and conversational agent: the coming face of the neural tier and the agentic layer, bound by the doctrine "a model proposes, only a governed actor commits". No product ships under that name today.

Zin built

The enterprise's constitutional compiler and agentic platform, in Zig: business logic, governance and organisational structure are compilation concerns. Commercial product, private repository.

Refine specification

Refinement-oriented programming: every change to code, interface or data becomes a governed, reversible, traceable refinement that a compiler validates before it lands. Today, a corpus of specifications and an interactive prototype.

Bangalo built

The practice: running many AI sessions on one body of work with git and a directory, without a framework. A doctrine of laws, each cited to the incident that paid for it. It is how Softanza builds Softanza. Never productised; publication of the repository to come.

The COBOL workbench ratified proposal

A workbench around unchanged COBOL estates: modern tooling and data access without a rewrite. Direction ratified by the author on 2026-08-16; the charter waits on its prerequisite. Its working name is held back until the prior-use search closes.

Softanza Studio specification

The commercial visual environment for authoring and operating declared worlds, over the same plain-text truth. Today, a design corpus and a browser prototype.

The rule of three lines

Every product boundary resolves with one cut. Capability, open: a mechanism with a conformance surface and no opinion about domain. Projection, open: how a declared world reaches people and machines: app, service, browser, device, constellation. Experience, commercial: the visual workbenches and the assurance behind the foundation. The language of the house is free; the houses built with it, and the guarantee behind the bricks, are the products.
